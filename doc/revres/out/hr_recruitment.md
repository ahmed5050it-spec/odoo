# hr_recruitment — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` +
> `extract_frontend.py`, key methods read from source, classification per the
> code-architect step.*

The **Recruitment** module (`hr_recruitment`, category *Human
Resources/Recruitment*, `application=True`) tracks a company's hiring pipeline:
job positions advertise openings, applicants flow through configurable kanban
stages, interviews are scheduled on the calendar, and the winning candidate is
converted into an employee. It is **not** auto-installed and is the recruitment
hub on top of `hr` — its center of gravity is `hr.applicant` (~60 fields, 55
methods), the document linking a candidate, a job, a stage, interviewers and the
resulting `hr.employee`.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `hr` | Extends `hr.job` and feeds `hr.employee`; recruitment is an HR add-on. |
| `calendar` | Interview scheduling — `hr.applicant.meeting_ids → calendar.event`. |

| `web_tour` | Ships an onboarding product tour (`hr_recruitment_tour`). |
| `digest` | Adds the "new colleagues" recruitment KPI to periodic digests. |

**Adds on top:** the source-to-hire pipeline (intake → stages → interview →
hire), email-alias application intake, talent pool with duplicate detection, and
recruitment analytics.

| `hr.recruitment.degree` / `hr.applicant.category` / `hr.job.platform` | Reference data | 2-3 | — |

**Central model:** `hr.applicant` — every other model references it. The module
also carries **14 `_inherit`-only extensions** (a mixin/extension when `_inherit`
has no `_name`): `calendar.event` (adds `applicant_id`), `hr.employee`,
`hr.department`, `res.partner`, `res.users`, `digest.digest`, `utm.*`, plus four
wizards (`applicant.send.mail`, `applicant.get.refuse.reason`,
`job.add.applicants`, `talent.pool.add.applicants`). The field set is
**relational-heavy**: `Many2one` (32) and `Many2many` (19) dominate alongside
many `Integer` (30) counters/KPIs — a pipeline tracker, not a money document.

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** **0** controller routes — there is no public/portal HTTP surface
  in core `hr_recruitment` (the careers website lives in the separate
  `website_hr_recruitment` module). It is a pure backend application.
- **Views:** **list (11)** and **form (9)** dominate, with **kanban (4)** for the
  stage pipeline plus pivot (2)/graph (2)/calendar (1)/activity (1) — an
  operational, kanban-driven recruiter UX with full BI analysis.
- **Security:** 31 access rules, **8 record rules**, 5 groups
  (`group_hr_recruitment_interviewer`, `group_hr_recruitment_user`,
  `group_hr_recruitment_manager`, `group_applicant_cv_display`, plus a
  `base.group_user` extension). Record rules notably scope **interviewers** to
  only the applicants on their jobs.

**Behavior metadata can't show (read from code):**
- **Email intake:** inbound mail to a job's alias auto-creates `hr.applicant`
  records (`message_new` + `hr.job._alias_get_creation_values`); `hr.job.platform`
  regex parses the candidate name out of job-board emails (e.g. LinkedIn).
- **Hire conversion:** `create_employee_from_applicant` builds an `hr.employee`,
  copies the applicant's unique attachments (CV) and back-links `applicant_ids`.
- **Pipeline mechanics:** moving into a `hired_stage` sets `date_closed` and
  decrements `hr.job.no_of_recruitment`; stages with a `template_id` post an
  automated email on entry (`_track_template`).
- **Interviews:** `action_create_meeting` creates a `calendar.event`; assigning
  `interviewer_ids` grants interviewer access and notifies them.
- **Dedup / talent pool:** applicants are matched across email/phone/LinkedIn
  (raw-SQL CTE) to power `application_count` and pool de-duplication.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Support activity — "support" value model.** `hr_recruitment` does not produce
or sell the firm's output; it supplies the organization with **human capital**.
There is no document flow to `stock`/`account` — the terminal output is a hired
`hr.employee`. It is therefore a cross-cutting **support** function (HR talent
acquisition) in Stabell & Fjeldstad's terms, an extension of the `hr` support
backbone rather than a primary chain/shop/network activity.

## APQC PCF Hint

**7.0 Develop and Manage Human Capital** — specifically **7.1 Recruit, source
and select employees** (post-and-source job openings, screen/interview
applicants, select and onboard). The hire boundary hands off into the rest of
7.0 via `hr.employee`.
