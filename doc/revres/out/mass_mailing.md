# mass_mailing — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` +
> `extract_frontend.py`, code-read behavior, and the `PROMPT.md` template.*

The **Email Marketing** module (`mass_mailing`, category *Marketing/Email
Marketing*, `application=True`) lets users design, send, schedule and **track**
mass emails. Its center of gravity is the `mailing.mailing` model (~73 fields,
91 methods) — the campaign document — paired with `mailing.trace`, the
per-recipient statistics record that captures delivery, opens, clicks, replies
and bounces. It depends on `mail` for delivery, `utm` for campaign attribution,
`link_tracker` for click tracking, `html_builder` for the editor, and `contacts`
for the audience.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `mail` | Actual sending goes through `mail.mail` / `ir.mail_server` (SMTP) and the incoming gateway for replies/bounces. |
| `utm` | Campaign / medium / source attribution; A/B variants share a `utm.campaign`. |
| `link_tracker` | Click tracking via shortened `/r/<code>` links and `link.tracker.click`. |
| `html_builder` | Drag-and-drop email body editor (OWL builder + themes). |
| `contacts` | Audience base: `mailing.contact` (lightweight, avoids bloating `res.partner`). |
| `digest`, `social_media`, `web_tour` | KPI/stats email layout, share buttons, onboarding tour. |

**Adds on top:** mailing lists & subscriptions, opt-out/blacklist compliance,
queued/scheduled sending, engagement tracking, A/B testing, and a statistics
report.

## Data Model (the ERM)

| Model (`_name`) | Description | #fields | Key relations |
|-----------------|-------------|--------:|---------------|
| **`mailing.mailing`** | Mass Mailing | 73 | `campaign_id→utm.campaign`, `contact_list_ids→mailing.list`, `mail_server_id→ir.mail_server`, `mailing_trace_ids→mailing.trace` |
| **`mailing.trace`** | Mailing Statistics | 20 | `mass_mailing_id→mailing.mailing`, `mail_mail_id→mail.mail`, `links_click_ids→link.tracker.click` |
| `mailing.contact` | Mailing Contact | 10 | `list_ids→mailing.list`, `subscription_ids→mailing.subscription`, `country_id→res.country` |
| `mailing.list` | Mailing List | 14 | `contact_ids→mailing.contact`, `mailing_ids→mailing.mailing` |
| `mailing.subscription` | Mailing List Subscription | 7 | `contact_id→mailing.contact`, `list_id→mailing.list`, `opt_out_reason_id→mailing.subscription.optout` |
| `mailing.trace.report` | Mass Mailing Statistics (SQL view) | 17 | — (read-only analytics) |

**Central model:** `mailing.mailing` (everything references it). `mailing.trace`
is kept in a separate table so emails can be deleted without losing statistics.
The module also *extends* `mail.mail`, `mail.thread`, `mail.blacklist`,
`link.tracker(.click)`, `utm.*` and `ir.mail_server` (17 inherit-only classes).

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** 17 — `auth='user'` backend (`/mailing/my`, `/mailing/<id>/view`,
  mobile preview) plus many **public** surfaces: unsubscribe/confirm/one-click,
  feedback & list-update jsonrpc, blocklist add/remove, and the two tracking
  endpoints `/mail/track/<id>/<token>/blank.gif` (open pixel) and
  `/r/<code>/m/<trace_id>` (click redirect). Public routes are HMAC-token gated.
- **Async send (code-read):** `action_launch` → `action_put_in_queue` sets
  `state='in_queue'` and triggers `ir_cron_mass_mailing_queue`;
  `_process_mass_mailing_queue` builds a `mail.compose.message` in
  `mass_mail` mode and pushes mails to the `mail.mail` outgoing queue (SMTP).
- **Tracking (code-read):** the open pixel calls `trace.set_opened`; the click
  redirect calls `link.tracker.click.add_click` → `set_opened` + `set_clicked`;
  the incoming gateway calls `set_replied` / `set_bounced`, with auto-blacklist
  after ≥5 bounces in 13 weeks.
- **A/B testing (code-read):** variants share a campaign with `ab_testing_pc`;
  `action_send_winner_mailing` sorts sent siblings by the chosen ratio metric and
  `action_select_as_winner` copies the winner at 100% and launches it.
- **Frontend (static):** 47 JS / 23 XML; OWL `MassMailingBuilder`,
  `MassMailingIframe`, `DesignTab`, `ThemeSelector`, `FieldMany2OneMailingFilter`.
- **Security:** 25 access rules, **1 record rule**, 2 groups
  (`group_mass_mailing_user`, `group_mass_mailing_campaign`).

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Primary model: Value Network (mediating), primary activity.** `mass_mailing`
is a network-style mediating activity (Stabell & Fjeldstad): it connects the
organization to a large audience at scale through mediating infrastructure —
mailing lists/subscriptions, SMTP delivery, UTM campaigns and open/click/bounce
tracking. It performs the network firm's **network promotion** function
(recruiting and serving a contact network) rather than transforming inputs
(chain) or solving a bespoke client problem (shop). It is a **primary** activity
— the marketing broadcast channel — not internal support. Its `utm` +
`link_tracker` ties feed attribution back into the value network.

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill (`.claude/skills/run-odoo/`). With the server up and an
`-i mass_mailing` database:

```python
# odoo shell
mailings = env['mailing.mailing'].search([], limit=5)
print(mailings.mapped(('subject', 'state', 'sent', 'opened', 'clicked')))
# status breakdown of traces:
print(env['mailing.trace'].read_group([], ['__count'], ['trace_status']))
# send a test:
#   m.action_test()  -> opens mailing.mailing.test wizard
# open pixel (public): GET /mail/track/<mail_id>/<token>/blank.gif -> 1x1 gif
```
