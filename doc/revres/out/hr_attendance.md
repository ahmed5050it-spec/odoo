# hr_attendance — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the code-explorer + code-architect workflow: facts from
> `extract_module.py`/`extract_frontend.py` + behavioral reads of
> `models/hr_attendance.py`, `models/hr_employee.py`, `controllers/main.py`,
> and `static/src/public_kiosk/public_kiosk_app.js`.*

The **Attendances** module (`hr_attendance`, category *Human Resources/Attendances*,
`application=True`) records when employees clock in and out and derives their
worked and overtime hours. Its defining surface is a **standalone public kiosk
OWL app** plus a backend systray widget, both funnelling into one create-or-close
toggle on `hr.attendance`. It depends on `hr` (employees), `barcodes` (badge
scanning), and `base_geolocalize` (GPS/IP stamping of each punch). It is an HR
**support** activity: it captures a labour-presence fact and feeds it to
HR/payroll — it sells nothing and starts no document chain.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `hr` | Provides `hr.employee`/`hr.department`, the worker the attendance is recorded against. |
| `barcodes` | Badge-scan capture at the kiosk (`employee_barcode_scanner` action + barcode bus). |
| `base_geolocalize` | `base.geocoder` reverse-geocodes the check-in/out latitude/longitude or IP into a location string. |

**Adds on top:** check-in/out events, calendar/lunch-aware worked-hours computes,
a configurable overtime ruleset engine with approval, two housekeeping crons
(auto check-out, absence detection), and a token-gated public kiosk.

## Data Model (the ERM)

| Model (`_name`) | Description | #fields | Key relations |
|-----------------|-------------|--------:|---------------|
| **`hr.attendance`** | Attendance (check-in/out event) | 29 | `employee_id→hr.employee`, `linked_overtime_ids→hr.attendance.overtime.line` |
| `hr.attendance.overtime.line` | Overtime computed per day/period | 11 | `employee_id→hr.employee`, `rule_ids→…overtime.rule` |
| `hr.attendance.overtime.rule` | Overtime Rule (timing/rate/tolerance) | 18 | `ruleset_id→…overtime.ruleset`, `resource_calendar_id→resource.calendar` |
| `hr.attendance.overtime.ruleset` | Overtime Ruleset (per company/country) | 8 | `rule_ids→…overtime.rule`, `company_id→res.company` |
| `hr.employee` *(extension)* | Employee | 17 | `attendance_ids`, `last_attendance_id`, `overtime_ids`, `attendance_manager_id` |

**Central model:** `hr.attendance` — `mail.thread`-tracked, `_order` by
`check_in desc`. Field mix is **measurement + provenance heavy**: 8 `Float`
(worked/overtime/expected hours, lat/long), `in_*`/`out_*` `Char`/`Selection`
provenance (mode kiosk/systray/manual/technical, IP, browser, location). The
overtime sub-tree (line/rule/ruleset) is the policy engine; `hr.employee` is
extended with 17 attendance fields (`attendance_state`, `hours_today`, …).

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** 13 total (5 representative in evidence). 2 HTTP: the public
  `/hr_attendance/<token>` kiosk page (website, sitemap) and the `user`
  `/hr_attendance/kiosk_mode_menu/<company_id>`. The rest are JSON-RPC — mostly
  `auth='public'` (barcode scan, manual selection, employee infos, set badge,
  create employee, settings) plus `auth='user'` systray check-in/out and
  keepalive. Public routes are token-gated by `res.company.attendance_kiosk_key`.
- **Views:** form (3), list (7), kanban (2), search (3), **pivot (1) / graph
  (1)** — operational lists plus light analytics.
- **Security:** 11 access rules, **10 record rules**, **5 groups**
  (`group_hr_attendance_own_reader`, `group_hr_attendance_officer`,
  `group_hr_attendance_user`, `group_hr_attendance_manager` + the Attendances
  privilege). Officers are scoped to employees where they are
  `attendance_manager_id`; base users may only read their own attendances.
- **Frontend:** 13 JS / 11 OWL components. The kiosk is a **standalone OWL App**
  (`kioskAttendanceApp`) mounted on the public page via `new App(...).mount(
  document.body)`, served by the `hr_attendance.assets_public_attendance` bundle
  (30 assets) — not a backend client action.

### Behavior beyond metadata (code-read)
- `hr.employee._attendance_action_change` — the one toggle: **creates** an open
  `hr.attendance` (check_in=now) or **closes** the open one (check_out=now),
  stamping `in_*`/`out_*` geo; raises if checking out with no open record.
- `_compute_worked_hours` localizes to the employee calendar tz and **subtracts
  lunch intervals** (fixed schedules) via `Intervals` algebra — not a raw delta.
- `create/write/unlink → _update_overtime` **rebuilds overtime lines** from the
  version's ruleset on every check_in/check_out/employee change.
- `_compute_attendance_state` + `_compute_presence_state` make a checked-in
  employee read `hr_presence_state='present'` (2nd priority after login).
- Crons: `_cron_auto_check_out` closes forgotten punches past expected+tolerance
  (`out_mode='auto_check_out'`); `_cron_absence_detection` inserts `technical`
  1-second attendances to book negative overtime for unjustified absences.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Support activity (firm infrastructure / HR management), value model =
support.** `hr_attendance` produces no saleable output and triggers no
stock/account document chain; it records a **labour-presence fact** and derives
worked & overtime hours that downstream HR/payroll consume. Per Stabell &
Fjeldstad it is a support activity enabling the workforce, not a primary
value-creating one. The dependency set is pure enabling infrastructure — `hr`
core, `barcodes` for capture, `base_geolocalize` for stamping — with no link
into a primary value flow.

## APQC PCF Hint

**7.0 Develop and Manage Human Capital** — specifically the *manage employee
time & attendance* sub-process within workforce administration. The overtime
ruleset/approval edge also touches the reward/compliance side of 7.0 (validated
extra hours feeding pay), but the module itself stays in workforce time
management.

## How to Drive It

Use the **run-odoo** skill (`odoo shell`):

- `e = env['hr.employee'].search([], limit=1); e._attendance_action_change(); (e.attendance_state, e.hours_today)`
  — toggle a punch and read state + today's hours.
- `env['hr.attendance'].search([], limit=5).mapped(('employee_id.name','check_in','check_out','worked_hours','overtime_hours'))`
  — inspect attendances and computed hours.
- `env.company.attendance_kiosk_url` — the token-gated public kiosk URL.
- `curl -s http://localhost:8069/hr_attendance/<attendance_kiosk_key>` — the
  public kiosk page (auth=public).
