# purchase — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` + code-read
> behavioral notes + the `PROMPT.md` template.*

The **Purchase** module (`purchase`, category *Supply Chain/Purchase*,
`application=True`) turns requests-for-quotation into confirmed purchase orders
and drives downstream vendor billing. It is **not** auto-installed and sits
surprisingly low in the graph: it depends only on `account`. Its center of
gravity is the `purchase.order` / `purchase.order.line` pair (~44 / ~42 fields) —
the document that links a vendor, products, pricing, and the resulting vendor
bill. Where `sale` is the demand-side front end of the chain, `purchase` is its
**supply-side procurement support** activity.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `account` | Pulls in accounting so confirmed POs can create vendor bills (`account.move`, `in_invoice`) and reconcile them. The *only* hard dependency. |

**Adds on top:** the RFQ→PO→bill lifecycle, vendor (`product.supplierinfo`)
auto-registration, a configurable PO approval / double-validation policy, a
PO-to-bill matching (3-way-match) view, a vendor portal, and a purchase analysis
report. Optional extensions are toggled via settings (`module_purchase_requisition`,
`module_account_3way_match`, `module_purchase_product_matrix`).

## Data Model (the ERM)

| Model (`_name`) | Description | #fields | Key relations |
|-----------------|-------------|--------:|---------------|
| **`purchase.order`** | Purchase Order | 44 | `partner_id→res.partner` (vendor), `order_line→purchase.order.line`, `invoice_ids→account.move` |
| **`purchase.order.line`** | Purchase Order Line | 42 | `order_id→purchase.order`, `product_id→product.product`, `invoice_lines→account.move.line` |
| `purchase.report` | Purchase Analysis Report | 27 | `partner_id→res.partner`, `order_id→purchase.order` (SQL view model) |
| `purchase.bill.line.match` | PO-line / bill-line matching view | 21 | `pol_id→purchase.order.line`, `aml_id→account.move.line` |
| `purchase.bill.union` | Purchases & Bills Union | 9 | `vendor_bill_id→account.move`, `purchase_order_id→purchase.order` |
| `bill.to.po.wizard` | Bill-to-PO wizard | 2 | `purchase_order_id→purchase.order` |
| `account.move` *(extension)* | — | 6 | `purchase_id→purchase.order` (adds purchase links to bills) |

**Central model:** `purchase.order` (everything references it). `account.move`
here has a `_name` but is really an **extension** of the accounting model. The
field profile is relational and amount-centric — `Many2one` (55) dominates,
followed by `Float`/`Monetary` (46 combined) and `Selection`/`Boolean` (35:
state and feature flags).

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** 5, a **vendor portal** surface — `/my/rfq*` and `/my/purchase*`
  (vendor reviews/updates an RFQ, downloads EDI). The portal-facing read/update
  routes are `auth='public'` (token-scoped); the listing routes are `auth='user'`.
- **Views:** form (3), **list (8)**, kanban (2), plus pivot (2)/graph (2)/
  calendar/activity — an operational, analyst-friendly module.
- **Security:** 35 access rules, **8 record rules**, 5 groups
  (`group_purchase_user`, `group_purchase_manager`, `group_warning_purchase`,
  `group_send_reminder`).

## Key Behavior Beyond Metadata (code-read)

- **`button_confirm`** validates each line has a product, **auto-registers the
  vendor** on the product (`product.supplierinfo`, capped at 10 sellers), then
  either approves directly (→ `state='purchase'`, `date_approve=now`) or routes
  to **`'to approve'`**.
- **`_approval_allowed` (double validation):** `po_double_validation='one_step'`
  always allows; `'two_step'` allows only below `po_double_validation_amount`
  (currency-converted); purchase managers always bypass — the configurable PO
  approval threshold policy.
- **`action_create_invoice`** builds `account.move` records with
  `default_move_type='in_invoice'`, groups by (company, partner, currency), and
  flips negative-total moves to refunds — the PO→**vendor bill** side-effect.
- **State machine:** `draft (RFQ) → sent → to approve → purchase → done/cancel`.
  `button_cancel` blocks if the PO is locked or has live vendor bills.
- **SQL view models:** `purchase.report`, `purchase.bill.union`,
  `purchase.bill.line.match` are read-only analytics/reconciliation views.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Value model: Support activity.** `purchase` is a **procurement support**
activity in Stabell & Fjeldstad terms — it provisions *inputs* (sources goods
and services from vendors) so the primary chain activities (`stock`, `mrp`,
`sale`) can run. It is not a primary transform: its only dependency is `account`
and its terminal output is a vendor bill (`account.move`), not a delivered
customer product. (With `purchase_stock` installed, confirmation also spawns
`stock.picking` receipts — a chain hand-off — but core `purchase` is support.)
