# mass_mailing_sms — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` +
> `extract_frontend.py`, code-read behavior, and the `PROMPT.md` template.*

The **SMS Marketing** module (`mass_mailing_sms`, category *Marketing/Email
Marketing*, `application=True`, not auto-installed) adds an **SMS channel** to
the Email Marketing engine. Rather than introduce a new campaign document, it
extends `mailing.mailing` with a `mailing_type='sms'` and reuses the whole
mass_mailing machinery — audience selection, the queue, A/B testing and the
statistics report — swapping only the delivery substrate from SMTP/`mail.mail`
to `sms.sms` sent over Odoo's IAP SMS gateway. It sits on `mass_mailing` (the
engine) and `sms` (the gateway), with `portal` for the public unsubscribe pages.
Most of its 1,873 Python LOC are `_inherit` extensions (9 of 11 models).

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `mass_mailing` | The campaign engine: `mailing.mailing`, `mailing.trace`, the send queue cron, A/B testing, and the statistics report are all reused. |
| `sms` | The delivery gateway: `sms.sms`, `sms.composer`, `sms.tracker` and the IAP transport (`https://sms.api.odoo.com`) + `/sms/status` webhook. |
| `portal` | Public-facing website context for the SMS unsubscribe / opt-out pages. |

**Adds on top:** an `sms` mailing type, SMS body/template editing with a
link-aware character counter, per-recipient SMS tracking, public SMS opt-out /
phone-blacklist routes, SMS A/B testing, and IAP credit/account handling.

| `mailing.contact` *(extend)* | Adds `mobile` + `mail.thread.phone` | 1 | — |

Only two concrete models are new (`mailing.sms.test`, plus `mailing.contact`
which also mixes in `mail.thread.phone`); the rest are `_inherit` extensions —
this is a **bridge/extension module**, not a standalone subsystem. Field types
are relational-light and flag-heavy (Many2one 7, Boolean 5, Selection 5,
Integer 5): mostly mode switches (`sms_force_send`, `sms_allow_unsubscribe`) and
IAP failure flags (`sms_has_insufficient_credit`, `sms_has_unregistered_account`).

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes (3, all `auth='public'`, `type=http`):** `/sms/<mailing>/<code>` and
  `/sms/<mailing>/unsubscribe/<code>` render the unsubscribe pages (validate the
  trio code/mailing/number, then list-opt-out or phone-blacklist); `/r/<code>/s/<sms_id>`
  is the click-tracking short-link redirect that resolves a click to its trace.
- **Send path (code-read):** `_action_send_mail` is overridden to split off
  `mailing_type='sms'` records → `action_send_sms` → `sms.composer` in mass mode
  → `sms.sms` rows (each nesting a `mailing.trace` + `sms.tracker`). Sending is
  either inline (`sms_force_send`) or queued: the `sms.sms` create triggers
  `ir_cron_sms_scheduler_action` → `_process_queue` → IAP batch send (**this is
  where SMS credits are consumed**). Delivery reports flow back via `sms.tracker`
  → `mailing.trace.trace_status`, flipping the mailing to `done`.
- **Views:** form 2 / list 3, no own pivot/graph — SMS variants of the
  mailing/trace forms plus an SMS mailing-list view; analytics reuse the parent
  `mass_mailing` statistics report.
- **Security:** 0 own groups, 0 record rules, 3 ACLs — all reuse
  `mass_mailing.group_mass_mailing_user`. It piggybacks on the engine's posture.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Value model: `network` · activity class: `primary` · APQC: 3.0 Market and
Sell Products and Services.**

This is the SMS channel of the `mass_mailing` **value network** and inherits its
classification (Stabell & Fjeldstad): a mediating activity that connects the
firm to a large audience at scale through mediating infrastructure — mailing
lists/subscriptions, the SMS/IAP gateway, UTM campaigns, and per-recipient
delivery/click/bounce tracking. It broadcasts to a contact network rather than
transforming inputs (chain) or solving a client problem (shop). It is **primary**
(unlike the base `sms` gateway, which is cross-cutting *support* infrastructure)
because here SMS *is* the marketing campaign: the module adds a campaign document
type, a marketing audience/opt-out model, and A/B testing on top of the gateway.

## APQC PCF Hint

**3.0 Market and Sell Products and Services** — specifically *3.4 Develop and
manage marketing communications*: SMS is the execution channel for marketing
campaigns, the SMS sibling of the email broadcast in the parent module.

## How to Drive It

Use the **run-odoo** skill (`odoo shell`) against the real model names:

```python
# SMS campaigns and their funnel
env['mailing.mailing'].search([('mailing_type','=','sms')], limit=5) \
    .mapped(('subject','state','expected','delivered','clicked'))
# SMS trace status distribution
env['mailing.trace'].read_group([('trace_type','=','sms')], ['__count'], ['trace_status'])
# outgoing SMS tied to mailings (state / failure_type)
env['sms.sms'].search([('mailing_id','!=',False)], limit=5).mapped(('number','state','failure_type'))
```

Curl the public surface: `GET /sms/<mailing_id>/<trace_code>` renders the SMS
unsubscribe page for that trace; `GET /r/<code>/s/<sms_id_int>` 301-redirects and
records a `link.tracker.click` against the trace. (Live IAP sending needs an
activated database with SMS credits.)
