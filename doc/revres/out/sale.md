# sale — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` + the
> `PROMPT.md` template. Example output.*

The **Sales** module (`sale`, category *Sales/Sales*, `application=False`) turns
quotations into confirmed sales orders and drives downstream invoicing. It is
**not** auto-installed and sits mid-graph: it depends on `sales_team`,
`account_payment`, and `utm`, and is in turn one of the most depended-on hubs in
the codebase. Its center of gravity is the `sale.order` / `sale.order.line` pair
(~65 fields each) — the document that links a customer, products, pricing, and
the resulting invoice.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `sales_team` | Salesperson / sales-team (`crm.team`) ownership and routing of orders. |
| `account_payment` | Pulls in accounting + payment so confirmed orders can invoice and collect. |
| `utm` | Campaign/source/medium tracking on orders (marketing attribution). |

**Adds on top:** the quotation→order→invoice lifecycle, a product/combo
**configurator** (web routes), pricing/discount wizards, and a sales analysis
report.

## Data Model (the ERM)

| Model (`_name`) | Description | #fields | Key relations |
|-----------------|-------------|--------:|---------------|
| **`sale.order`** | Sales Order | 65 | `partner_id→res.partner`, `company_id→res.company`, `pending_email_template_id→mail.template` |
| **`sale.order.line`** | Sales Order Line | 67 | `order_id→sale.order`, currency/company (related) |
| `sale.report` | Sales Analysis Report | 39 | `partner_id→res.partner`, `pricelist_id→product.pricelist` (a SQL view model) |
| `sale.advance.payment.inv` | Advance Payment Invoice (wizard) | 12 | `sale_order_ids→sale.order` |
| `sale.order.discount` | Discount Wizard | 6 | `sale_order_id→sale.order` |
| `sale.mass.cancel.orders` | Mass-cancel wizard | 3 | `sale_order_ids→sale.order` |
| `account.move` *(extension)* | — | 6 | `team_id→crm.team` (adds sales links to invoices) |

**Central model:** `sale.order` (everything references it). `account.move` here
has a `_name` but is really an **extension** of the accounting model (adds sales
fields). The model set is **relational-heavy** — `Many2one` (66) dominates,
followed by `Boolean` (54, lots of state/feature flags) and `Monetary`/`Float`
(57 combined: a pricing/amount-centric document).

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** 14, all `auth='user'`, mostly `type='jsonrpc'` — a backend web
  surface for the **product/combo configurator**
  (`/sale/product_configurator/*`, `/sale/combo_configurator/*`). No public
  storefront here (that's `website_sale`).
- **Views:** form (5), **list (9)**, kanban (5), plus pivot/graph/calendar/
  activity (1 each). List-heavy with full BI views — an operational,
  analyst-friendly module.
- **Security:** 47 access rules, **27 record rules**, 4 groups — a high
  record-rule count reflecting multi-company + salesperson/own-documents
  visibility scoping.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Primary model: Value Chain (transform), front-end link.** `sale` is the
*Marketing & Sales* primary activity of the chain — it converts demand into a
confirmed order that flows downstream to delivery (`stock`) and invoicing
(`account`). The `account_payment` dependency wires it to the cash end of the
chain. It is a **primary** activity, not support. (For service businesses the
same `sale.order` feeds the **shop** model via `sale_timesheet`/`sale_project`,
and the storefront variant feeds the **network** model via `website_sale` — but
core `sale` itself is chain front-end.)

## APQC PCF Hint

**3.0 Market and Sell Products and Services** — specifically the "sell" /
manage-sales-orders process groups. The advance-payment and discount wizards
touch 9.0 (Manage Financial Resources) at the boundary.

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill (`.claude/skills/run-odoo/`). With the server up and an
`-i sale` database:

```python
# odoo shell
orders = env['sale.order'].search([], limit=5)
print(orders.mapped('name'), orders.mapped('state'))
# the configurator surface:
#   curl .../sale/product_configurator/get_values  (auth=user, jsonrpc)
print(env['sale.order.line'].fields_get(['price_subtotal','product_uom_qty']).keys())
```
