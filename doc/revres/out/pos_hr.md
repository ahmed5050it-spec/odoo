# pos_hr — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow (code-explorer + code-architect): facts from
> `extract_module.py` / `extract_frontend.py` + code-read of the models and the
> OWL frontend. Odoo 19.0. Upgraded from BASELINE to DEEP.*

The **POS - HR** module (`pos_hr`, category *Sales/Point of Sale*,
`auto_install=True`, not an application) is the thin **link module between Point
of Sale and HR**. It lets *employees* — not Odoo users — operate a till: an
unlimited number of `hr.employee`s can log on to one shared register by **barcode
badge and/or PIN**, and every sale, payment and cash move they make is stamped
with their `employee_id`. It adds only 3 real models and **no routes**; its real
weight is 877 Python LOC plus an OWL/JS layer (`static/src`) that patches the
offline POS terminal. It sits directly on its two dependencies, `point_of_sale`
(the till it augments) and `hr` (the employee identity it borrows).

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why |
|------------|-----|
| `point_of_sale` | The subsystem it extends — `pos.config`/`pos.session`/`pos.order`/`pos.payment` gain an employee dimension; the OWL terminal gains a login/switch layer. |
| `hr` | Supplies `hr.employee` (with `barcode`, `pin`, `user_id`, `work_contact_id`) as the cashier identity, replacing per-cashier Odoo users. |

**Adds on top:** cashier badge/PIN authentication, mid-shift cashier switching,
tiered per-till access (minimal/basic/advanced employee lists), per-employee
sales/cash attribution, role-based action gating in the POS, and a per-cashier
Z-report. Toggled by the `module_pos_hr` flag on `pos.config`.

## Data Model (the ERM)

| Model (`_name` / `_inherit`) | Role | New fields |
|------------------------------|------|-----------|
| `hr.employee` *(+`pos.load.mixin`)* | Cashier identity loaded into the client | — (adds load methods: domain/fields/read, barcode+PIN hashing) |
| `pos.config` *(+`hr.mixin`)* | Till profile + who may log in | `minimal_employee_ids`, `basic_employee_ids`, `advanced_employee_ids` (M2m) |
| `pos.session` *(inherit)* | Currently logged-in cashier | `employee_id` (M2o, tracking) |
| `pos.order` *(inherit)* | Owning cashier | `employee_id` (M2o), `cashier` (Char, stored compute) |
| `pos.payment` *(inherit)* | Cashier (related from order) | `employee_id` (related, stored, indexed) |
| `account.bank.statement.line` *(inherit)* | Cash-move author | `employee_id` (M2o) |
| `report.pos.order` *(inherit)* | Analysis dimension | `employee_id` |
| `report.pos_hr.single_employee_sales_report` | Single-employee Z-report | — |

All meaningful models are **`_inherit` extensions** (mixin/bolt-on, no new
`_name` except the report) — `pos_hr` is a pure bridge. The field mix is
**relational-heavy** (7 Many2many + 5 Many2one) with 1 Char and 1 Boolean: it
adds *links to employees*, not new attributes.

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** none. `pos_hr` exposes no web/RPC surface of its own; it rides the
  `point_of_sale` terminal and the generic `pos.load.mixin` data-load contract.
- **Frontend (gap #3 — the headline):** **14 JS / 7 XML**, only **1 new
  component** (`CashierSelectionPopup`) and **12 patches** of existing POS OWL
  classes. The reusable hook `useCashierSelector`
  (`utils/select_cashier_mixin.js`) drives badge login (a `useBarcodeReader`
  `cashier` handler matching `SHA1(barcode)`, then a PIN prompt) and manual
  selection. `LoginScreen`, `PosStore`, `Navbar`, `OrderPaymentValidation`,
  `CashierName`, `PosOrder` are patched to force a login before selling, persist
  the connected cashier in `sessionStorage`, buffer cashier→session writes while
  offline, and gate actions by role. None of this is in Python metadata.
- **Views:** zero of every type in the facts — the config UI is grafted onto the
  parent `pos.config`/settings forms via XML, not new view records.
- **Security:** **0 access rules, 0 record rules, 0 groups** — it reuses
  `point_of_sale.group_pos_user` / `group_pos_manager` and enforces access in
  Python (`get_barcodes_and_pin_hashed`, `_employee_domain`) and the OWL client.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Value model: chain. Activity class: support.** `pos_hr` augments a value-chain
primary activity — the retail **checkout** realized by `point_of_sale` — but is
itself a **support** overlay: it neither sells, delivers nor posts accounting. Its
entire contribution is the **staffing/identity layer**: who is allowed to operate
the till, who is currently operating it, who owns each order/payment, and what
each role may do. It is a workforce/identity enabler *around* the sale, not the
sale. (Contrast the parent `point_of_sale`, classified chain/**primary**.)

## APQC PCF Hint

**3.0 Market and Sell Products and Services** — retail operations, **cashier
management**. The capability it adds (manage who staffs and operates the counter,
and attribute takings to them) sits on the in-store sell process; it has no
inventory (4.0) or finance (9.0) logic of its own beyond stamping `employee_id`
on cash-move and report rows.

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill with a DB that has `pos_hr` installed and
`module_pos_hr` enabled on a `pos.config`:

```python
# odoo shell
cfg = env['pos.config'].search([], limit=1)
print(cfg.name, cfg.module_pos_hr, cfg.basic_employee_ids.mapped('name'))
print(env['hr.employee'].search(cfg._employee_domain(env.uid)))   # who may log in
print(env['pos.order'].search([('employee_id','!=',False)], limit=5)
        .mapped(('name','cashier','employee_id')))
print(env['hr.employee'].search([],limit=3).get_barcodes_and_pin_hashed())  # SHA1 only
# launch /pos/ui/<config_id> -> opens on LoginScreen demanding a badge scan / PIN
```
