# website_sale_loyalty — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` +
> behavioral code-reads + the `PROMPT.md` template.*

The **Coupons, Promotions, Gift Card and Loyalty for eCommerce** module
(`website_sale_loyalty`, category *Website/Website*, `application=False`) is the
**promotions layer of the online storefront**. It is `auto_install` whenever both
`website_sale` and `sale_loyalty` are present, and depends on `website_sale`,
`website_links` and `sale_loyalty`. It owns almost no data of its own — its job is
to **wire the loyalty rule engine into the live `/shop` cart**: enter a promo or
coupon code, auto-apply discounts, claim rewards, share coupon links, and spend
gift cards / eWallets at checkout. The rule-matching and point math live upstream
in `sale_loyalty`/`loyalty`; this module is the eCommerce channel adapter.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `website_sale` | The storefront cart/checkout it patches — overrides `pricelist`, `cart`, delivery, payment and the `sale.order`/`sale.order.line` cart logic. |
| `sale_loyalty` | The order-side engine (`_try_apply_code`, `_update_programs_and_rewards`, `_apply_program_reward`) it reuses; this module only adds website scoping. |
| `website_links` | The `link.tracker` shortener behind shareable `/coupon/<code>` campaign links (`coupon.share`). |

**Adds on top:** website-scoped promotion programs (`ecommerce_ok`), the cart
coupon-entry/claim/share controllers, gift-card & eWallet checkout flows, and a
website-builder toggle for the promo-code box.

**Center of gravity:** the `sale.order` extension (21 methods) — the cart is where
all promotion behaviour is realized. Only `coupon.share` and the two
`loyalty.program` booleans are genuinely new fields; everything else is an
`_inherit` extension. `python_loc` ≈ 2612, `xml_loc` ≈ 441.

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** public storefront entry points — `pricelist` *(override)* is the
  coupon-entry endpoint, `/coupon/<string:code>` the shareable link, and
  `/shop/claimreward` the per-reward "Claim" button; plus `/wallet/top_up`
  (`auth=user`) and a `cart` override. No backend web API.
- **Views:** 1 form only (the `coupon.share` wizard) — UI lives in inherited
  `website_sale` storefront templates, not new view types.
- **Security:** **1** access rule (the wizard), **0** record rules, **0** groups —
  it inherits website + loyalty scoping rather than defining its own.

Metadata cannot show what the cart actually does. Code-reads establish:

- **Code application** — `pricelist` calls `sale.order._try_apply_code(promo)`,
  which resolves the code to a `loyalty.rule` or `loyalty.card` under a
  **website-scoped** domain (this module swaps the `sale_ok` leaf for
  `ecommerce_ok` and AND-adds a `website_id` filter), **row-locks** the
  `loyalty_program` (`FOR UPDATE NOWAIT`), and rejects loyalty/eWallet from code
  entry. `/coupon/<code>` stashes a `pending_coupon_code` in the session and
  `_try_pending_coupon()` **replays** it on the next cart recompute.
- **Recompute on every change** — `_recompute_cart`, `_verify_cart_after_update`,
  the `cart` controller and the delivery hooks all call
  `_update_programs_and_rewards()` + `_auto_apply_rewards()`; reward/discount lines
  are a **derived projection** of programs × cart, never stored statically.
  Deleting a reward line records it into `disabled_auto_rewards` to stop re-claim;
  `_compute_website_order_line` merges per-tax discount lines into one display row.
- **Reward claim** — `/shop/claimreward` validates against
  `_get_claimable_and_showable_rewards()` (which also surfaces the partner's
  nominative loyalty/gift cards with enough unexpired points) and applies the
  chosen reward, handling multi-product choice and re-rating free-over shipping.
- **Gift card / eWallet at checkout** — `/wallet/top_up` adds the eWallet trigger
  product to the cart; `PaymentPortal._validate_transaction_for_order` re-applies
  programs immediately before payment and **raises** if the total moved (guards a
  changed/expired gift card). Free-shipping rewards discount the carrier line.
- **Frontend** — 4 JS files, **0 OWL**: two `public.interaction`s (`CouponToaster`
  notifications, `GiftCardCopy` clipboard), a `Checkout.prototype` patch that
  live-updates discount/shipping amounts in the cart summary, and a website-builder
  `coupon_option` plugin to toggle the promo box.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Value model: Network (primary).** Unlike the parent `loyalty` (a cross-cutting
rule **engine**, classified *support*), this module operates **inside the live
two-sided storefront** — shoppers ↔ the merchant's catalogue — and exists to
**drive conversion**. It mediates the buy decision with promo codes, auto-applied
discounts, claimable rewards, shareable campaign links and stored-value tender
(gift cards / eWallets). That price-mediation and switching-cost creation is
value-**network** logic (Stabell & Fjeldstad), and because it acts directly in the
customer-facing sell channel it is a **primary** activity, not firm
infrastructure — hence **network / primary**.

## APQC PCF Hint

**3.0 Market and Sell Products and Services** — the *manage promotions / pricing*
process group, applied at the **online point of sale**. This is distinct from the
3.5 order-management groups owned by `website_sale`/`sale`: the discount only lands
on an `account.move` line downstream (9.0), but this module's own subject matter is
**promotion delivery at checkout**.

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill (`.claude/skills/run-odoo/`). With the server up and an
`-i website_sale_loyalty` database:

```python
# odoo shell
progs = env['loyalty.program'].search([('ecommerce_ok', '=', True)])
print(progs.mapped(('name', 'program_type', 'website_id')))
so = env['sale.order'].search([('website_id', '!=', False)], limit=1)
print(so._try_apply_code('YOURCODE'))          # returns {coupon: rewards} or {'error'/'not_found'}
# shareable link wizard:
print(env['coupon.share'].create_share_action(program=progs[:1]))
```

```bash
curl -s -c cj 'http://localhost:8069/coupon/TESTCODE?r=/shop'   # share-link entry (public)
# then POST /shop/claimreward with reward_id (+ product_id for multi-product)
```
