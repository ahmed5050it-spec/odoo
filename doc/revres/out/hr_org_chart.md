# hr_org_chart — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` +
> `extract_frontend.py` + targeted code-read of the controller and the two
> inherited employee models.*

The **HR Org Chart** module (`hr_org_chart`, category *Human Resources*,
`application=False`) is a thin org-visualization add-on: it renders an
employee's **managers-up / subordinates-down** reporting hierarchy on the
employee form (and the related public/portal view) and adds a dedicated
hierarchy view of the whole tree. It **auto-installs with `hr`** and depends on
`hr` plus `web_hierarchy`. It defines **no business model of its own** (0
`_name`) — it only `_inherits` `hr.employee` and `hr.employee.public` to add
computed hierarchy fields, and ships the actual chart as a **client-side OWL
component**.

## Behavior & Surfaces

Code-read findings metadata cannot show:

- **Recursive subordinate closure.** `_get_subordinates` walks `child_ids`
  recursively with a `parents` accumulator so a manager who is also a member of
  a sub-department under their own report (CEO in R&D under the CTO) is
  **excluded from their own subordinate set** — cycle-safe. `child_all_count =
  len(subordinate_ids)`; `child_count` (direct) is one batched `_read_group`.
  All computes are `compute_sudo=True` so counts show even when the user can't
  read the underlying records.
- **`/hr/get_org_chart` route.** Loads the employee as `hr.employee.public`
  (gated on `has_access('read')`), walks `parent_id` upward bounded by
  `_managers_level=5` to build the `ancestors` manager chain (cycle-breaking),
  returns `{self, managers (reversed/capped + managers_more), children}` with
  per-node id/name/job, `/mail/view` link and direct/indirect sub-counts.
- **Access-aware routing.** `/hr/get_redirect_model` picks `hr.employee` vs
  `hr.employee.public` per read access; `/hr/get_subordinates` returns
  direct / indirect / all subordinate ids for drill-down.
- **Frontend (gap #3).** The chart is **entirely client-side OWL**: `HrOrgChart`
  (the `hr_org_chart` form widget) + `HrOrgChartPopover` (per-node hover) under
  `static/src/fields`, plus a `hr_employee_hierarchy` card/renderer view under
  `static/src/views`; `HrOrgChart` uses `useRecordObserver` to refetch on
  `parent_id`/`resId` change and calls `/hr/get_org_chart`. Not in Python metadata.

## Fit-to-Standard

- **Out of box:** interactive org-chart widget on the employee form; recursive
  direct/indirect subordinate counts; `hr_employee_hierarchy` tree view;
  access-aware rendering via `hr.employee.public`; cycle-safe recursion.
- **Typical fits:** show an employee their reporting line/team; let HR explore
  span of control without custom reporting; auto-enabled wherever Employees runs.
- **Common gaps:** no matrix/dotted-line reporting (single `parent_id`); no
  headcount/FTE roll-up analytics; no printable/exportable org chart;
  manager-chain depth fixed at 5 (`_managers_level`, not per-company config).
- **Drive hints:** `odoo shell` →
  `env['hr.employee'].browse(1)._get_subordinates().mapped('name')`; POST to
  `/hr/get_org_chart` with `{employee_id, context:{max_level:5}}` in an
  authenticated session.
