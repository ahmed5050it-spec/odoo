# pos_self_order — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow (code-explorer + code-architect): facts from
> `extract_module.py` / `extract_frontend.py` + code-read of the models &
> controllers. Odoo 19.0.*

The **POS Self Order** module (`pos_self_order`, category *Sales/Point Of Sale*,
`application=False`) lets customers order for themselves — "view the menu on
their smartphone." It is **auto-installed with `pos_restaurant`** and layers a
*second, customer-facing* OWL single-page app on top of the cashier's Point of
Sale terminal, across three channels: a read-only **QR digital menu**
(`consultation`), **mobile QR ordering** (`mobile`), and an on-site **self-service
kiosk** (`kiosk`). It depends on `pos_restaurant` (tables, presets, combos),
`http_routing` (public routes), and `link_tracker` (short QR URLs). It owns
almost no value logic of its own — every order it captures is synced into the
**existing** `pos.session` run by the host POS.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `pos_restaurant` | Reuses `restaurant.table` / `restaurant.floor` (per-table QR), presets, courses, combos; auto-installs alongside it. |
| `http_routing` | Serves the self-order SPA on **public** `website=True` routes (`/pos-self/<config_id>`). |
| `link_tracker` | Wraps each self-order URL in a short, trackable `link.tracker` link for QR codes. |

**Adds on top:** a public self-order OWL app (menu → cart → pay → track), QR-code
generation (per-table or generic, PNG+SVG zip), kiosk self-payment at a terminal,
and the plumbing that injects customer orders into the live cashier session with
real-time bus notifications.

| `pos.order.line` *(extension)* | Order line | +1 | `combo_id→product.combo` |

**Only one own model** (`pos_self_order.custom_link`); the rest are **`_inherit`
extensions** (16 inherit-only models per facts) that bolt self-order fields onto
`pos.config`, `pos.order(.line)`, `pos.preset`, `restaurant.table`, plus
`pos.load.mixin` data-loading hooks. The field mix is **Selection/flag-heavy**
(modes, service modes, pay-after) rather than relational — fitting a *channel
configuration* layer, not a transaction owner. The center of gravity is
`pos.config`, the fat terminal profile, which here gains the whole
`self_ordering_*` channel definition.

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** 13 paths, **all `auth="public"`** (anonymous diners). Entry
  `/pos-self/<config_id>` (http, `website=True`) renders the SPA; `/pos-self/data`
  & `/pos-self/relations` (jsonrpc) ship the menu payload;
  `/pos-self-order/process-order/<device_type>/` submits an order;
  `/kiosk/payment/<pos_config_id>/<device_type>` drives terminal payment;
  `validate-partner`, `remove-order`, `get-user-data`, `get-slots`,
  `change-printer-status`, `kiosk/increment_nb_print`, and `/pos-self/ping` round
  it out. Public access is constrained by an **access_token** (`consteq` checks)
  and by acting as `self_ordering_default_user_id`.
- **Frontend (gap #3 — the headline):** a **separate** OWL app from the cashier
  POS — entry `static/src/app/self_order_index.js`, `root.js`, its own
  `router.js`, with `pages/ services/ store/ models/` — bundled in
  `pos_self_order.assets` (56 entries): **54 JS / 37 XML / ~28 components**
  (LandingPage, ProductListPage, ProductPage, ComboPage/ComboStepper, CartPage,
  PaymentPage, ConfirmationPage, OrdersHistoryPage, plus `self_order`, `router`,
  `printer` client services). It **patches** the cashier app
  (`PosStore`/`PosOrder`/`PosData`/`TicketScreen`) so self-orders surface in the
  till. None of this customer journey is reverse-engineerable from Python.
- **Views & security:** essentially no backend UI (1 list view); **2 access
  rules, 0 record rules, 0 new groups** — it reuses `point_of_sale.group_pos_*`.

## Value-Configuration Classification

- **value_model: `network`** (activity_class **primary**). Against Stabell &
  Fjeldstad, this is **mediating technology**, not a chain. The parent
  `point_of_sale` owns the sell→deliver→invoice conversion; `pos_self_order`
  adds **no order/accounting flow of its own** — `process_order` builds a
  sanitized dict and calls `pos.order.sync_from_ui` to drop the order into the
  **already-open `pos.session`**, then `recompute_prices()` re-prices it
  server-side. Its real contribution is *connecting a new party (the diner)
  directly to the POS service*: a public OWL app, a **QR / access-token +
  `link.tracker` + `table_identifier` access layer**, tiered membership
  (consultation / mobile / kiosk), and a **longpolling bus** (`config._notify`)
  linking customer terminals to cashier and kitchen in real time — the
  value-network pattern. *Alternative considered:* `chain` (orders flow into the
  chain-style POS pipeline) — **rejected** because this module federates a
  channel onto the host rather than performing the conversion itself.

## APQC PCF Hint

**3.0 Market and Sell Products and Services.** Self-ordering is a customer-facing
**sales / order-capture channel** for the point-of-sale family (consistent with
the `apqc_odoo_map` sell-family rows and the `point_of_sale` parent record); the
fulfilment (4.0) and cash/AR (9.0) steps happen downstream in the host POS.

## How to Drive It

Use the **run-odoo** skill. Real handles from the facts:

```python
# which tills expose a self-order channel and in what mode
env['pos.config'].search([('self_ordering_mode','!=','nothing')]) \
    .mapped(('name','self_ordering_mode','self_ordering_service_mode'))

# the short QR URL (built via link.tracker)
env['pos.config'].browse(ID)._get_self_order_url()

# customer-sourced orders, by channel
env['pos.order'].search([('source','in',('kiosk','mobile'))], limit=5) \
    .mapped(('name','source','state'))
```

Routes: `curl` jsonrpc **`/pos-self/ping`** with `{'access_token': cfg.access_token}`
→ `{'response':'pong'}`; or browse **`/pos-self/<config_id>`** to launch the
customer SPA.
