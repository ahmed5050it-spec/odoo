# pos_sale — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` /
> `extract_frontend.py` + targeted code-read of `addons/pos_sale/models` and
> `static/src`. DEEP record.*

The **POS - Sales** module (`pos_sale`, category *Sales/Point of Sale*,
`application=False`, **`auto_install=True`**) is the bridge between the Point of
Sale and the Sales backend. It defines no model of its own: it only extends
existing ones (`sale.order`/`sale.order.line` pull in `pos.load.mixin`, plus
`pos.order`, `pos.order.line`, `pos.config`, `pos.session`, `crm.team` and the
`sale.report` SQL view). Its job is to let a cashier **load and settle an
existing sale order/quotation at the till** (or take a **down-payment** on it),
keep delivery/invoicing reconciled across both channels, and surface POS sales
in standard sales reporting per Sales Team. It auto-installs whenever both
`point_of_sale` and `sale_management` are present.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `point_of_sale` | The terminal app + `pos.order`/`pos.config`/`pos.session` it settles orders into; provides `pos.load.mixin` and the OWL store it patches. |
| `sale_management` | The `sale.order`/`sale.order.line` documents being settled, and the down-payment machinery it extends. |

**Adds on top:** loading a draft SO into the active POS order, settle vs.
down-payment flows, cross-channel `qty_delivered`/`qty_invoiced`/`amount_unpaid`
reconciliation, a `crm.team` link on every till, and a UNION of POS lines into
`sale.report`.

## Data Model (the ERM)

| Model (`_name`) | Description | #fields | Key relations |
|-----------------|-------------|--------:|---------------|
| `sale.order` *(ext + `pos.load.mixin`)* | adds `amount_unpaid`, `pos_order_count`, `pos_order_line_ids` | 3 | `pos_order_line_ids→pos.order.line` |
| `sale.order.line` *(ext + `pos.load.mixin`)* | adds POS back-link | 1 | `pos_order_line_ids→pos.order.line` |
| `pos.order` *(ext)* | adds `crm_team_id`, `currency_rate`, `sale_order_count` | 3 | `crm_team_id→crm.team` |
| `pos.order.line` *(ext)* | adds the SO links + `qty_delivered`, `down_payment_details` | 4 | `sale_order_origin_id→sale.order`, `sale_order_line_id→sale.order.line` |
| `pos.config` *(ext)* | adds `crm_team_id`, `down_payment_product_id` | 2 | `crm_team_id→crm.team`, `down_payment_product_id→product.product` |
| `pos.session` *(ext)* | `crm_team_id` (related to config) | 1 | `crm_team_id→crm.team` |
| `crm.team` *(ext)* | `pos_config_ids` inverse | 1 | `pos_config_ids→pos.config` |
| `sale.report` *(ext)* | UNION POS lines into the SQL view | 2 | `order_reference→pos.order` |

All entries are `_inherit`-only **extensions/mixins** (no new `_name`); the
field-type mix is **relational-heavy** (8 Many2one, 3 One2many) — pure linkage
glue rather than new attributes. `account.move(.line)`, `product.template`,
`res.partner`, `res.config.settings`, `stock.picking` get smaller extensions too.

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** none — the module exposes no new web/RPC surface; the settle flow
  rides the existing POS `sync_from_ui` / `pos.load.mixin` plumbing.
- **Views:** none of its own (0 form/list/kanban); all UI is OWL patches.
- **Security:** 1 access rule (`crm.team` writable by `group_pos_manager`) +
  1 record rule; 0 new groups.
- **Behavior (code-read):** `PosStore._processSaleOrder` offers *Settle* /
  *down-payment %* / *down-payment fixed*; settling copies SO lines into
  `pos.order.line` (UoM `read_converted` s2p) with `sale_order_origin_id` +
  `sale_order_line_id` back-links. `pos.order.sync_from_ui` (override) turns a
  down-payment line into a real `sale.order.line`
  (`_create_down_payment_lines_from_base_lines`), confirms draft/sent SOs, and
  **reduces waiting `stock.picking` demand** so the warehouse won't ship twice.
  `SaleOrderLine._prepare_qty_delivered/_prepare_qty_invoiced` fold POS qty back
  onto the SO; `sale.order.amount_unpaid` exposes the residual to pay.
- **Frontend (gap #3):** no new OWL components/services — **7 patches**
  (`PosStore`, `PosOrder`, `PosOrderline`, `TicketScreen`, `ControlButtons`,
  `OrderPaymentValidation`, `DataServiceOptions`) in `point_of_sale._assets_pos`
  (7 JS / 2 XML). The settle popup and Quotation/Order button live here, unseen
  by Python metadata.
- **Integrations (gap #7):** none (no HTTP/SDK/api-keys).

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Value model: `chain` — primary activity.** `pos_sale` realizes the retail
Sell/Deliver step of the value chain by settling sales-chain orders at the
counter, then folding the POS payment, delivery and invoicing back onto the
originating `sale.order` so the chain closes once. The downstream deliver
(stock picking-demand reduction) and AR (invoicing, `amount_unpaid`) effects are
triggered consequences, not its mission. Having no value logic beyond linking
two chain activities, it inherits the `chain`/`primary` class of both parents
(`sale`, `point_of_sale`) rather than `shop`/`network`.

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill (`-i pos_sale` database, server up):

```python
# odoo shell — tills wired to a team + down-payment product
env['pos.config'].search([]).mapped(lambda c: (c.name, c.crm_team_id.name, c.down_payment_product_id.name))
# SOs that have been touched at the POS
env['sale.order'].search([('pos_order_count','>',0)], limit=5).mapped(('name','state','amount_unpaid'))
# POS lines linked back to a sale order
env['pos.order.line'].search([('sale_order_origin_id','!=',False)], limit=5).mapped(lambda l: (l.sale_order_origin_id.name, l.qty, l.qty_delivered))
# POS rows in the unified sales analysis, per team
env['sale.report'].read_group([('order_reference','like','pos.order%')], ['price_total:sum'], ['team_id'])
```
