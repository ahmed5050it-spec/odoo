# mrp_account — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the code-explorer + code-architect workflow: facts from

> `models/{mrp_production,stock_move,analytic_account,product,mrp_workorder,mrp_workcenter,account_move}.py`,
> `wizard/mrp_wip_accounting.py`, `report/stock_valuation_report.py`.*

The **Accounting - MRP** module (`mrp_account`, category *Supply Chain/Manufacturing*,
v1.0, `application=False`, **`auto_install=True`**) is the **cost-accounting bridge**
between the manufacturing value chain (`mrp`) and the financial system of record
(`account` via `stock_account`). It owns almost no documents of its own (one transient
WIP wizard) and **0 HTTP routes**; instead it *extends* `mrp.production`,
`mrp.workorder`, `mrp.workcenter`, `stock.move`, `account.move`, `account.analytic.*`
and `product.*` so that completing a manufacturing order rolls its component, labour
and overhead cost into **stock valuation** and the **general ledger**, and tracks
work-order cost on the **analytic (cost-centre) ledger**. It installs automatically
whenever both `mrp` and `stock_account` are present — the glue is invisible until you
turn on automated inventory valuation.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why |
|------------|-----|
| `mrp` | The subject: `mrp.production`, `mrp.workorder`, `mrp.workcenter`, `mrp.bom` — the cost is rolled up off these. |
| `stock_account` | The valuation engine: `stock.valuation.layer`, `_get_value_from_production`, `get_product_accounts`, the stock journal/valuation accounts this module posts into. |

**Provides on top:** the manufacturing cost roll-up (`_cal_price`), the labour-cost GL
posting (`_post_labour`), production-move valuation (`_get_value_from_production`),
analytic work-order cost lines, BoM standard-cost computation, kit valuation handling,
and the WIP period-cut wizard. A `post_init_hook` (`_configure_journals`) wires the
default production journals.

### Behavior beyond metadata (code-read)
- **`mrp.production._cal_price` — the cost roll-up.** After `super()` values the
  consumed component moves, it adds `Σ work_order._cal_cost()` (labour =
  duration × `workcenter.costs_hour`) and `extra_cost × qty`, giving
  `total_cost = Σ component value + workcenter cost + extra cost`. It splits that onto
  byproducts by `cost_share %` and sets `price_unit` on the finished `move_finished_ids`.
  Only fifo/average products get the rolled cost (standard-cost keeps `standard_price`).
  This `price_unit` is exactly what `stock_account` writes into the finished-goods
  **`stock.valuation.layer`**.
- **`_post_inventory → _post_labour` — labour into the GL.** Once stock posts the
  component-consumption and finished-goods valuation layers, MOs reaching state `done`
  (real-time valuation + a production-location valuation account) get a single balanced
  `account.move` on the product's `stock_journal`: **debit** each
  `workcenter.expense_account_id` (fallback product expense), **credit** the production
  location's `valuation_account_id`. Each line is written back onto the workorder's
  `mrp.workcenter.productivity.account_move_line_id` (idempotency guard skips if already
  linked) — capitalising workcenter cost into the produced goods.
- **`stock.move._get_value_from_production` — production-output valuation.** On
  production moves it returns `value = quantity × price_unit` with an MO-scoped
  description, the hook `stock_account` uses to amount the finished/byproduct valuation
  layer. `_get_kit_price_unit` / `_get_all_related_sm` value phantom-BoM (kit) outputs
  by exploding the kit and summing component prices so kits are not double counted.
- **`mrp.workorder._create_or_update_analytic_entry` — cost-centre tracking.** On
  `_compute_duration`/`_set_duration` it computes `-(duration/60) × costs_hour` and,
  when the workcenter carries an `analytic_distribution`, creates/updates
  `account.analytic.line` rows (`category='manufacturing_order'`, hours UoM,
  `ref=production.name`) on `wc_analytic_account_line_ids`, unlinked on cancel/unlink —
  posting shop-floor labour onto the analytic ledger **independently** of the GL.
- **WIP wizard (`confirm`) — period-cut accounting.** For in-progress MOs it posts a
  WIP entry (credit valuation account for picked component value, credit overhead for
  workorder cost, debit `account_production_wip_account_id`) and **immediately books a
  reversing entry** on the next day, so WIP appears on the balance sheet only at the
  cut-off; both moves carry `wip_production_ids`.
- **`product._compute_bom_price` / `_set_price_from_bom` — standard cost.** The *Compute
  Price from BoM* button rolls operation costs + component `standard_price × qty`
  (recursing child BoMs, netting byproduct `cost_share`) into the product
  `standard_price`. `product.category.property_stock_account_production_cost_id` is the
  shared valuation counterpart account.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Support bridge — value-model `support`, activity_class `support`.** `mrp_account`
is **not a primary transform**: it never moves or converts material (that is `mrp` +
`stock`). It records the *financial consequences* of manufacturing — valuing the
finished-goods/byproduct/kit `stock.valuation.layer`, posting labour cost to the GL,
feeding the analytic cost-centre ledger, and recognising WIP at period close. Under
Stabell & Fjeldstad / Porter it is **firm-infrastructure support**, the glue between
the *make* value chain (`mrp`, value-model `chain`) and the financial system of record
(`account`/`stock_account`, also `support`). Its `auto_install=True` onto `mrp` +
`stock_account` confirms it as a passive bridge, not a standalone capability.

## APQC PCF Hint

**9.0 Manage Financial Resources** — specifically **9.3 Perform cost / management
accounting** (analytic manufacturing cost) and **9.x product costing & inventory
valuation**. It *feeds*, rather than performs, the **4.3 Produce/Manufacture** primary
activity its parent `mrp` owns (`apqc_odoo_map.tsv` rows 4.3.1.4 / 4.3.1.7); the cost
postings themselves are support, consistent with the map classing `account.move`
receivable/payable as **support** under 9.x.

## How to Drive It

Use the **run-odoo** skill (`odoo shell`):

- `mo = env['mrp.production'].search([('state','=','done')], limit=1); mo.move_finished_ids.mapped(('price_unit','quantity'))`
  — the rolled finished-goods cost.
- `odoo shell --log-handler=odoo.sql_db:DEBUG`: `mo.button_mark_done()` — trace
  `_cal_price` + `_post_labour` (labour `account.move`) + finished-goods
  `stock.valuation.layer`.
- `env['account.analytic.line'].search([('category','=','manufacturing_order')], limit=5).mapped(('name','amount','account_id.display_name'))`
  — workcenter analytic cost lines.
- `p = env['product.product'].search([('bom_count','>',0)], limit=1); p.button_bom_cost(); p.standard_price`
  — BoM standard-cost roll-up.
- No `curl` surface: the module exposes **0 routes**.
