# hr — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` +
> `extract_frontend.py` + targeted code-read of the key models.*

The **Employees** module (`hr`, category *Human Resources/Employees*,
`application=True`) is Odoo's HR foundation: it centralizes the **employee**,
**department**, **job position** and dated **version/contract** master data that
every other HR app (attendance, time-off, payroll, recruitment, skills) builds
on. It is **not** auto-installed and depends on `base_setup`, `digest`,
`phone_validation`, `resource_mail` and `web`. Its center of gravity is the
`hr.employee` model (~102 fields), which is a `resource.mixin` and `_inherits`
the `hr.version` model to delegate job/department/wage/contract fields.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `resource_mail` | Pulls `resource` (calendars, `resource.resource`) + `mail` (chatter, activities). |
| `phone_validation` | International formatting of work/mobile phone numbers. |
| `digest` | Periodic KPI digests (e.g. new-hire counts). |
| `base_setup` / `web` | Config settings and the OWL backend UI. |

**Adds on top:** the employee directory with a private/public confidentiality
split, department org-chart, job positions with headcount targets, an
employee-version/contract snapshot history, user-account provisioning, and
presence/onboarding/departure tooling.

## Data Model (the ERM)

| Model (`_name`) | Description | Key relations |
|-----------------|-------------|---------------|
| **`hr.employee`** | Employee | `resource_id→resource.resource`, `user_id→res.users`, `work_contact_id→res.partner`, `parent_id→hr.employee`, `_inherits hr.version` |
| **`hr.employee.public`** | Public Employee | `department_id→hr.department`, `job_id→hr.job`, `employee_id→hr.employee` |
| **`hr.department`** | Department | `parent_id→hr.department`, `manager_id→hr.employee`, `member_ids→hr.employee` |
| **`hr.job`** | Job Position | `department_id→hr.department`, `employee_ids→hr.employee` |
| **`hr.version`** | Version / contract snapshot | `employee_id→hr.employee`, `job_id→hr.job`, `structure_type_id→hr.payroll.structure.type` |
| **`hr.work.location`** | Work Location | `address_id→res.partner`, `company_id→res.company` |

15 models total; no public HTTP routes. Security: 26 access rules, 13 record
rules, 2 groups (`group_hr_user`, `group_hr_manager`).

## Behavior & Surfaces

Code-read findings metadata cannot show:

- **Private/public split.** `hr.employee` overrides `_search`, `fetch`,
  `search_fetch`, `get_view`, `get_formview_id`: a user without
  `hr.group_hr_user` is transparently rerouted to `hr.employee.public` (only
  non-sensitive fields). Private fields carry `groups="hr.group_hr_user"`;
  `_check_private_fields` raises `AccessError` otherwise.
- **User linkage.** `action_create_users` creates `res.users` from employees
  (login = work_email); `_sync_user` keeps partner/image/tz aligned; a unique
  `(user_id, company_id)` constraint forbids duplicates.
- **Org-chart.** `parent_id`/`child_ids` form the manager hierarchy; changing a
  department's `manager_id` cascades `parent_id` to all members;
  `get_department_hierarchy` feeds the OWL **DepartmentChart** widget.
- **Versioning.** `hr.employee` `_inherits hr.version`; `create_version` /
  `create_contract` snapshot dated versions; a cron keeps `current_version_id`
  current.
- **Archive/departure.** `action_archive` clears dangling links and opens the
  departure wizard; a cron schedules contract/work-permit expiry reminders.

**Frontend (gap #3):** 36 JS files, 6 OWL components (DepartmentChart,
