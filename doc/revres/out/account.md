# account — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` +
> behavioral code-reads + the `PROMPT.md` template.*

The **Invoicing / Accounting** module (`account`, category *Accounting/Accounting*,
`application=True`) is the **financial system of record** for the whole Odoo
suite. It is **not** auto-installed but is a deep hub: it depends only on
foundational modules (`base_setup`, `onboarding`, `product`, `analytic`,
`portal`, `digest`) yet almost every operational module (sale, purchase, stock,
expense, payroll, every `l10n_*`) depends on it to write its financial
consequences into a double-entry ledger. Its center of gravity is the
`account.move` / `account.move.line` pair — the universal "journal entry" that is
simultaneously invoice, vendor bill, payment move, and manual entry.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `product` | Invoice/bill lines reference products, prices, UoM. |
| `analytic` | Cost/analytic distribution on journal items (`analytic.mixin`). |
| `portal` | Customer self-service invoice portal (`/my/invoices`). |
| `digest` | Periodic KPI digests over financial figures. |
| `base_setup` / `onboarding` | Company + accounting onboarding/setup wizards. |

**Adds on top:** the chart of accounts, journals, taxes & fiscal positions, the
invoice/bill lifecycle, payments & reconciliation, bank statements, and a
declarative financial-reporting engine.

**Central model:** `account.move` — 57 models in the module, but everything
orbits the move/line ledger. The field profile is unusually large
(`account.move` alone has 142 fields, 353 methods; `account.move.line` 146
methods): heavy `Many2one` (283) relational structure, `Boolean` (223 — state
and feature flags) and `Monetary` (56) reflecting an amount-centric ledger.

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** 12. A customer **portal** (`/my/invoices`, `/terms`, auth user/
  public), secure attachment downloads (`/account/download_invoice_*`), and the
  product-catalog JSON-RPC surface (`/product/catalog/*`). No public storefront.
- **Views:** form (25), **list (32)**, kanban (10), search (19), pivot (2),
  graph (3), activity (1) — a list-and-BI-heavy operational accounting module.
- **Security:** 145 access rules, **31 record rules**, **10 groups** — the
  richest access model of the core hubs, layering invoice/user/manager/readonly/
  secured roles plus multi-company scoping.

The metadata cannot show what *posting* means. Reads of the code establish:

- `account.move._post / action_post` — draft→posted assigns the legal sequential
  number (and `inalterable_hash` on secured journals), validates the entry is
  balanced and complete, and freezes hash-locked fields; gated on
  `group_account_invoice`.
- `account.payment.action_post` + `_synchronize_to_moves` — a payment is a facade
  over a balanced `account.move`; posting drives `draft→in_process→paid` and
  keeps the journal entry's outstanding + liquidity lines in sync.
- `account.move.line.reconcile` / `_reconcile_plan` — matches debit/credit lines
  into `account.partial.reconcile` (then `account.full.reconcile`); cross-currency
  matches spawn exchange-difference moves. This is how invoices become "paid".
- `button_draft` enforces legal immutability (blocked for hash-secured entries,
  detaches the generated PDF, drops analytic lines).
- `account.report` is a declarative engine: report-line **expressions** hold
  formulas evaluated at render time — the numbers are not stored fields.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Value model: Support (Porter support activity / financial backbone).**
Unlike `sale` (a primary value-chain activity), `account` does not create
customer value directly — it **records the financial consequences** of the
primary activities into the general ledger. Sale produces a customer invoice,
purchase a vendor bill, stock a valuation entry; `account` is where all of those
land as `account.move`. It is the firm infrastructure / accounting support
activity, classified **support / support**.

## APQC PCF Hint

**9.0 Manage Financial Resources.** Concretely (per `apqc_odoo_map.tsv`):
**9.2.x Process accounts receivable** — `account.move` customer invoice
`action_post`; and **9.5.x Process accounts payable** — `account.payment`
register payment. The general-ledger and reporting surface covers the broader
9.x "perform general accounting and reporting" process groups; tax handling
touches the statutory-reporting end of 9.0.

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill (`.claude/skills/run-odoo/`). With the server up and an
`-i account` database:

```python
# odoo shell
invs = env['account.move'].search([('move_type','=','out_invoice')], limit=5)
print(invs.mapped('name'), invs.mapped('state'), invs.mapped('payment_state'))
print(env['account.journal'].search([]).mapped(('name','type')))  # sale/purchase/bank/cash/general
# posting assigns the sequence:
#   inv = env['account.move'].create({...}); inv.action_post(); print(inv.name, inv.state)
# portal AR surface:  curl .../my/invoices  (auth=user)
```
