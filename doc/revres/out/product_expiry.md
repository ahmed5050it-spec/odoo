# product_expiry — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` +
> `extract_frontend.py`, behavior from code-read, narrative per `PROMPT.md`.*

The **Products Expiration Date** module (`product_expiry`, category *Supply
Chain/Inventory*, `application=False`) adds shelf-life management to Odoo
inventory. It is **not** auto-installed and is a lean leaf on the graph: it
depends only on `stock` and is depended on by nobody. It defines just **one**
new model — the `expiry.picking.confirmation` wizard — and otherwise works
entirely by **extending** `stock.lot`, `stock.move(.line)`, `stock.quant`,
`stock.picking`, `product.template/product` and the forecast/settings models
(11 `_inherit` extensions). Its center of gravity is the four dated fields it
hangs on every lot/serial: **expiration**, **best-before (use)**, **removal**
and **alert** dates.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `stock` | The only dependency. All expiry data rides on stock's lots, quants, moves and pickings; FEFO plugs into stock's removal-strategy hook. |

**Adds on top:** per-lot expiry/use/removal/alert dating computed from product
lead-times, the **FEFO** (First-Expiry-First-Out) removal strategy, an
**alert-date cron** that schedules activities, and a **delivery-confirmation
wizard** for expired stock.

## Data Model (the ERM)

| Model (`_name` / `_inherit`) | Role | #fields | Key relations |
|------------------------------|------|--------:|---------------|
| **`expiry.picking.confirmation`** | Confirm-expiry wizard (own model) | 4 | `lot_ids→stock.lot`, `picking_ids→stock.picking` |
| **`stock.lot`** *(ext)* | Lot/serial + 4 expiry dates | +7 | `use_expiration_date→product.product` |
| `stock.move.line` *(ext)* | Expiry/removal date per operation | +4 | `is_expired→stock.lot` |
| `stock.quant` *(ext)* | Fresh on-hand + FEFO order | +4 | `expiration_date/removal_date→stock.lot` |
| `product.template` *(ext)* | `use_expiration_date` + 4 lead-times | +5 | — |
| `stock.picking` *(ext)* | Expired-lot delivery guard | +0 | — |

Field mix is **date/flag-centric**: 8 `Datetime` (the dates), 10 `Boolean`
(feature/state flags), 4 `Integer` (product lead-times). 1,743 Python LOC / 450
XML LOC. No `_name` model carries a SQL view here — everything is stored
extensions plus one transient wizard.

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Date computation:** on lot creation `expiration_date = now +
  product.expiration_time`; `use/removal/alert_date` are derived by subtracting
  the product's `use_time/removal_time/alert_time`. Editing the expiration date
  shifts the others by the same delta (`_compute_dates`). *(code-read)*
- **FEFO:** `stock.quant._get_removal_strategy_order('fefo')` returns
  `'removal_date, in_date, id'` — reservations pick the earliest-expiring stock
  first; quants past their `removal_date` report `available_quantity = 0`.
- **Delivery guard:** `stock.picking._pre_action_done_hook` detects expired /
  past-removal move lines and pops `expiry.picking.confirmation`; the user
  confirms (`process`) or strips the expired lines (`process_no_expired`).
- **Alert cron:** `stock.lot._alert_date_exceeded` finds internally-stored lots
  past `alert_date`, schedules a To-Do activity on the product responsible, and
  marks each lot reminded-once.
- **Routes:** none (0). **Views:** no own form/list views in facts (extends
  stock's via inheritance + report/wizard XML). **Security:** 1 access rule, 0
  record rules, 1 group (`group_expiry_date_on_delivery_slip`).
- **Frontend:** 2 JS files patching `ForecastedDetails`/`ForecastedHeader` —
  sort the Forecasted report by `removal_date` and net out to-remove quantities
  so fresh-vs-expiring stock is legible. No OWL components, no integrations.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Value Chain (transform), support augment.** A warehousing/distribution firm
is a **value chain** (Stabell & Fjeldstad); inventory is its inbound/outbound
logistics. `product_expiry` does not own a process or expose routes — it
**augments stock** with dating, FEFO ordering and expiry alerting. So it is a
**support** activity riding the chain's logistics leg, not a primary one. (Tag:
`value_model = chain`, `activity_class = support`.)

## APQC PCF Hint

**4.0 Deliver Physical Products** — specifically **4.3.2 inventory management**
(manage logistics and warehousing). FEFO and the fresh-quantity logic refine how
stock is reserved and counted; the alert cron touches the quality/compliance
edge of warehouse operations.

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill (`.claude/skills/run-odoo/`) with an `-i product_expiry`
database:

```python
# odoo shell
tmpls = env['product.template'].search([('use_expiration_date','=',True)], limit=5)
print(tmpls.mapped(('name','expiration_time','removal_time')))
lots = env['stock.lot'].search([('expiration_date','!=',False)], limit=5)
print(lots.mapped(('name','expiration_date','removal_date','product_expiry_alert')))
print(env['stock.quant']._get_removal_strategy_order('fefo'))  # 'removal_date, in_date, id'
```
