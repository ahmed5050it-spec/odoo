# stock — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` + key-model
> code-reads, narrated per the `PROMPT.md` template.*

The **Inventory** module (`stock`, category *Supply Chain/Inventory*,
`application=True`, v1.1) is Odoo's logistics engine: it physically receives,
stores, moves, reserves and ships goods. It is a heavyweight hub — **51 models**,
~45k Python LOC, ~10k XML LOC — and depends only on `product`,
`barcodes_gs1_nomenclature` and `digest`, yet is depended on by `sale`,
`purchase`, `mrp` and the whole supply chain. Its center of gravity is the
**move ledger**: `stock.picking` (transfer) → `stock.move` (planned) →
`stock.move.line` (executed) reconciled into `stock.quant` (on-hand balance).

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `product` | The thing being stocked — products, variants, UoM, categories. |
| `barcodes_gs1_nomenclature` | GS1 barcode parsing for lots/SSCC packages on labels. |
| `digest` | Periodic KPI digest hooks (inventory metrics). |

**Adds on top:** warehouses & location trees, the procurement/route engine
(`stock.rule`/`stock.route`), reservation & backorders, lot/serial traceability,
packages, putaway/removal strategies, reordering rules, inventory adjustments,
scrap and returns.

**Central model:** `stock.move` (130 methods, 76 fields) is the procurement and
flow engine; `stock.quant` (72 methods) is the on-hand truth. The field profile
is **relational and state-heavy** — `Many2one` (208) dominates, then `Boolean`
(136, feature/state flags) and `Selection` (55, state machines).

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Stock is an append-only ledger, not a mutable balance.** Levels never get
  edited in place: `stock.quant._update_available_quantity` is the single sudo
  entry point, and inventory adjustments (`_apply_inventory`) generate
  *compensating* `stock.move` records to a loss location, validate them, then
  re-trigger reservation (code-read).
- **`button_validate` is the workhorse.** It confirms drafts, sanity-checks,
  runs pre-action wizards, then `_action_done`; on partial fulfillment it splits
  demand and creates a **backorder** `stock.picking`, governed by
  `picking_type.create_backorder` (never/ask/always) (code-read).
- **`stock.rule` is the procurement brain.** `_run_pull` turns a procurement
  into a sudo `stock.move` (MTS vs MTO vs `mts_else_mto`) and confirms it,
  recursively launching upstream procurements; `_run_push` applies push rules on
  arrival. This is how multi-step routes (input→QC→stock, pick→pack→ship) chain
  and how upstream `sale`/`purchase`/`mrp` demand pulls a transfer into being
  (code-read).
- **Routes:** just 1 — an HTTP `auth='user'` report download
  (`/stock/<output_format>/<report_name>`). Stock is ORM/automation-driven, not
  a web-API surface.
- **Views:** **list (37)** and form (20) dominate, plus 9 kanban (picking-type
  dashboards) and pivot/graph for analytics — an operational, screen-heavy app.
- **Security:** 77 access rules, 16 record rules, **13 groups** (multi-location,
  multi-warehouse, lot tracking, owner tracking, advanced location…) — feature
  flags gate large slices of functionality.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Value Chain (transform/flow), primary activity.** `stock` is the **inbound and
outbound logistics** primary activity of Porter's value chain: goods physically
flow *through* it to the customer. The double-entry move ledger reconciled into
quant balances is textbook value-chain flow. It is **primary**, not support —
the product physically passes through it. (Valuation/accounting of that flow is
deliberately split out into `stock_account`.)

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill (`.claude/skills/run-odoo/`). With the server up and an
`-i stock` database:

```python
# odoo shell
# on-hand truth:
q = env['stock.quant'].search([('quantity','>',0)], limit=5)
print(q.mapped(('product_id.display_name','location_id.complete_name','quantity')))
# validate a ready transfer and watch the side-effects (move.line, quant, backorder):
p = env['stock.picking'].search([('state','=','assigned')], limit=1)
# p.button_validate()
# warehouse route config:
print(env['stock.warehouse'].search([]).mapped(('name','reception_steps','delivery_steps')))
```
