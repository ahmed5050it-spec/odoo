# account_payment — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` +
> behavioral code-reads + the `PROMPT.md` template.*

The **Payment - Account** module (`account_payment`, category *Accounting/
Accounting*, `application=False`, `auto_install` with **account**) is the **bridge**
that wires Odoo's online-payment engine to its accounting ledger. It depends on
exactly two modules — **`account`** (the AR/AP system of record) and **`payment`**
(the provider/transaction engine) — and exists only to join them: when an online
`payment.transaction` is confirmed it creates and reconciles an `account.payment`
against the customer invoice, and it lets customers pay posted invoices from the
portal. It owns a single real model (`payment.refund.wizard`); everything else is
eleven `_inherit` extensions that graft fields and methods onto existing models.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why |
|------------|-----|
| `account` | The AR ledger: `account.move` (invoices), `account.payment`, `account.journal`, reconciliation — what payments settle against. |
| `payment` | The cash rail: `payment.transaction` / `payment.provider` / `payment.token` state machine + acquirer link — the source of collection events. |

**Adds on top:** online invoice payment from the portal, automatic posting +
reconciliation of an `account.payment` from a confirmed transaction, pay-by-saved-
token in the backend, online refunds, and the provider→bank-journal wiring that
turns an online provider into a usable inbound payment method.

## Data Model (the ERM)

| Model (`_name`) | Kind | #fields | Key relations |
|-----------------|------|--------:|---------------|
| `payment.refund.wizard` | own model | 9 | `payment_id→account.payment`, `transaction_id→payment.transaction` |
| `payment.transaction` | _inherit | +3 | `payment_id→account.payment`, `invoice_ids→account.move` (m2m) |
| `account.move` | _inherit | +4 | `transaction_ids→payment.transaction`, computed `amount_paid` |
| `account.payment` | _inherit | +7 | `payment_transaction_id→payment.transaction`, `payment_token_id→payment.token`, `source_payment_id→account.payment` |
| `payment.provider` | _inherit | +1 | `journal_id→account.journal` (compute/inverse, domain type=bank) |
| `account.payment.method.line` | _inherit | +2 | `payment_provider_id→payment.provider` |
| `account.payment.register` | _inherit | +3 | `payment_token_id→payment.token` |
| `payment.link.wizard` | _inherit | +7 | installment / early-payment-discount fields |

Plus thin extensions of `account.journal`, `account.payment.method`,
`account.bank.statement.line`, `res.config.settings`. **No aggregate model** — the
center of gravity is the **m2m `account_invoice_transaction_rel`** that links
`payment.transaction.invoice_ids ↔ account.move.transaction_ids`. Field profile is
relation-heavy (Many2one/Many2many dominate) — a pure wiring module, not a
data-owning one (1 own model vs 11 inherits).

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** 5. Two JSON-RPC (public) collection endpoints
  (`/invoice/transaction/<id>`, `/invoice/transaction/overdue`), one HTTP portal
  page (`/my/invoices/overdue`), and overrides of payment's `/payment/pay` and
  account's `/my/invoices/<id>` to inject invoice context / a token guard.
- **Views:** **0 own views** — `xml_loc≈701` is all view *inheritance* into the
  invoice / provider / transaction / payment forms and portal & payment-form
  templates. UX surfaces inside existing accounting and portal screens.
- **Frontend (static):** 3 JS files, **no OWL**: two `public.interactions`
  (`portal_invoice_page_payment` opens the `#pay_with` modal on the portal invoice;
  `portal_my_invoices_payment_list`) and a `PaymentForm.prototype` patch.
- **Security:** 3 access rules, **1 record rule**, 0 groups — leans entirely on
  account's accounting groups and payment/portal record rules.

What metadata cannot show, established by reading the code:

- **`payment.transaction._post_process`** (override) — on `state=='done'` posts any
  draft `invoice_ids`, then calls **`_create_payment()`** (skipping validations /
  already-paid / partial-capture-source txs); logs the payment+tx link on the
  invoices; on `state=='cancel'` calls `payment_id.action_cancel()`.
- **`payment.transaction._create_payment`** — creates an `account.payment`
  (`amount=abs`, inbound/outbound by sign, **journal = `provider_id.journal_id`**,
  method-line filtered to the provider, token, `invoice_ids`), handles
  early-payment-discount write-offs, **`action_post()`s** it (→ a balanced
  `account.move`), records the one2one `payment_id`, then **reconciles** the
  payment and invoice lines on the receivable account — flipping `payment_state`
  to `in_payment`/`paid`.
- **`account.move`** — computed `amount_paid` = Σ transaction amounts in
  authorized/done; **`_has_to_be_paid`** gates the portal *Pay Now* dialog (feature
  flag `account_payment.enable_portal_payment`, posted `out_invoice`, residual>0,
  `payment_state` not_paid/in_payment/partial, no pending provider tx);
  `payment_action_capture`/`_void` act on authorized transactions.
- **provider↔journal wiring** — `payment.provider.journal_id` (compute/inverse)
  keeps an `account.payment.method.line` (`payment_provider_id`, journal,
  outstanding account) in sync via `_ensure_payment_method_line`; journals and
  method-lines refuse deletion while bound to an enabled/test provider.
- **controllers** — `/invoice/transaction/<id>` (public jsonrpc) creates the draft
  tx with `invoice_ids` preset; `account.payment.action_post` override spins up a
  `payment.transaction` + `_charge_with_token` for backend pay-by-saved-card.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Value model: Support (financial-management support extension).** This bridge
connects the cash/payment rail to the AR ledger; it is **not** a primary value
activity. Its core act — `_post_process → _create_payment → action_post +
reconcile` — records the financial consequence of a collection into the
double-entry ledger. It deliberately inherits **account's** support/support
classification, **not** payment's `network`: the value-network mediation
(onboarding acquirers, provisioning transactions, the `requests`-based acquirer
link) lives in the `payment` engine, while this module's own logic is ledger
record-keeping. Classified **support / support**.

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill. With an `-i account_payment` database (and a provider in
test state):

```python
# odoo shell — provider <-> journal wiring
env['payment.provider'].search([('state','in',('enabled','test'))]).mapped(('name','code','journal_id'))
env['account.payment.method.line'].search([('payment_provider_id','!=',False)]).mapped(('name','journal_id'))
# is an invoice payable online? what got settled?
inv = env['account.move'].search([('move_type','=','out_invoice'),('state','=','posted')], limit=1)
print(inv._has_to_be_paid(), inv.amount_paid, inv.payment_state)
tx = env['payment.transaction'].search([('invoice_ids','!=',False)], limit=1)
print(tx.reference, tx.state, tx.payment_id, tx.invoice_ids.mapped('payment_state'))
# portal collection:  curl jsonrpc /invoice/transaction/<invoice_id> (auth=public, needs invoice access_token)
```
