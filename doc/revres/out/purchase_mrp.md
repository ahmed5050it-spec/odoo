# purchase_mrp — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres code-explorer workflow: structural facts from
> `extract_module.py` / `extract_frontend.py` + behavioral facts read from

The **Purchase and MRP Management** module (`purchase_mrp`, category
*Supply Chain/Purchase*, `application=False`, **`auto_install=True`**) is the
three-way bridge that activates whenever both `mrp` and `purchase_stock` are
installed. Its manifest puts it plainly: it is "used when we want to keep track
of production orders generated from purchase order." It declares **no model of
its own** — 9 `_inherit`-only classes, ~3.0k Python LOC — and wires
`purchase.*` ↔ `mrp.*` ↔ `stock.*` across two largely independent concerns:
(1) a **bidirectional PO ↔ MO traceability link** (smart buttons that walk
procurement moves between a purchase order and the manufacturing order it feeds
or that generated it), and (2) **kit / phantom-BoM handling on purchase lines**
— buying a sellable kit explodes it into component moves and splits the
received qty, billed qty, and stock valuation across those components by
`cost_share`.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |

| `mrp` | Owns `mrp.production`, `mrp.bom`, `mrp.bom.line` — the manufacturing orders and phantom (kit) BoMs this module links to purchasing and cost-splits. |

`auto_install=True` means it activates automatically once both branches are

`cost_share` on BoM components, a "buy" route in the BoM-structure report, PO
lines surfaced in the MO Overview, and a vendor notification to the MO
responsible.

## Behavior & Surfaces

**Surfaces (metadata):**
- **Routes:** **0** — no web/RPC controller of its own.
- **Views:** **no new view records**; 4 inherited view files (~63 XML LOC) graft
  smart buttons / fields onto existing `purchase.order`, `mrp.production`,
  `mrp.bom`, and reordering-rule forms.
- **Security:** **2 ACLs**, **0 record rules**, **0 groups** — both ACLs grant
  `purchase.group_purchase_user` *read-only* on `mrp.bom` / `mrp.bom.line` so a
  buyer can see kit structure without MRP rights.
- **Frontend (static):** **1 JS file**, **0 OWL components**, patches
  `BomOverviewLine.prototype` (sourced from `mrp/static/src/**`) — the BoM
  Overview client line, to render the buy route. No outbound HTTP / SDK
  integrations (gap #7 empty).

**Code-read (the metadata gap — what the bridge actually does):**

- **PO ↔ MO is bidirectional and move-walked, not a stored FK.** Neither side
  stores a direct link; both counts traverse the procurement move graph.
  Forward: `purchase.order._get_mrp_productions()` =
  `(order_line.move_dest_ids | order_line.move_ids.move_dest_ids).raw_material_production_id`.
  Reverse: `mrp.production._get_purchase_orders()` rolls up
  `move_raw_ids(.move_orig_ids).created_purchase_line_ids` (PO **generated by**
  the MO's buy rule) **or** `.purchase_line_id` (PO that **supplies** the raw
  move) → `order_id`. The smart buttons (`action_view_*`) open form or list
  depending on count.
- **Buying a kit explodes it into component moves.** On receipt,
  `purchase.order.line._prepare_stock_moves` finds a `bom_type='phantom'` BoM for
  the line's (sale-linked) product, `explode()`s it, and stamps each generated
  move's `bom_line_id` with the matching component line. A PO line for a kit
  product therefore generates **N component `stock.move`s**, not one.
- **Received & billed qty are kit-aware.** `_prepare_qty_received` detects kit
  lines (`qty_received_method == 'stock_moves'` + a phantom BoM) and routes them
  through `_compute_kit_quantities_from_moves`, so a kit is "fully received" only
  when **all** components arrive in BoM proportion — not when the first move is
  done. `_get_qty_received_without_self` on `stock.move` mirrors this per move.
- **Cost is split across components by `cost_share`.** This is the heart of the
  module. `mrp.bom.line.cost_share` (% per component, validated to total 100 per
  variant by `_check_bom_lines`) drives `stock.move._get_cost_ratio`,
  `_get_value_from_bill`, and `_get_valuation_price_and_qty`: the single kit
  purchase price/value is apportioned to each component's stock valuation by its
  share. `_round_last_line_done` forces the last share to absorb rounding so the
  total lands exactly on 100. Without a defined share, `_get_cost_share` falls
  back to an equal `1 / component_count` split.
- **MO-triggered purchasing notifies the MO owner.** `stock.rule._notify_responsible`
  extends the standard buy-exception notice: when the procurement's group carries
  `mrp_production_ids`, it also notifies the MO's `user_id` (not just the product
  responsible) that a component PO needs attention.
- **Reports cross the boundary.** The BoM-structure report gains a `buy` route
  branch (vendor, `supplier.delay + rules_delay + days_to_purchase` lead time,
  min-qty alert); the MO Overview lists draft/sent/to-approve PO lines as
  "extra replenishments," apportioning each PO-line qty across the MOs its
  `move_dest_ids` roll up to.

baseline of `support`/`support`).** Under Stabell & Fjeldstad this is a pure
**value-chain** connector realizing **inbound logistics + operations**: it sits

manufacturing and apportioning landed kit cost are core supply-chain value
activities, even though the module owns no standalone business object — it only
inherits `purchase.*`, `mrp.*`, and `stock.*` to make the

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill (`.claude/skills/run-odoo/`). There are **0 routes to
curl** — drive it through `odoo shell` on the inherited models:

```python
# odoo shell — POs that sourced a manufacturing order (forward link)
po = env['purchase.order'].search([('mrp_production_count', '>', 0)], limit=1)
print(po.name, po.mrp_production_count, po._get_mrp_productions().mapped('name'))

# MOs that generated / are supplied by a purchase order (reverse link)
mo = env['mrp.production'].search([('purchase_order_count', '>', 0)], limit=1)
print(mo.name, mo._get_purchase_orders().mapped('name'))

# kit BoMs and their per-component cost split (must total 100 per variant)
kits = env['mrp.bom'].search([('type', '=', 'phantom')])
print(kits.bom_line_ids.mapped(('product_id.display_name', 'cost_share')))
```

A buyer (`purchase.group_purchase_user`) can read kit BoMs but not edit them
(the two read-only ACLs) — verify by reading `mrp.bom` as a purchase-only user.

## Open Questions

- **Sale-linked kit path.** `_prepare_stock_moves` calls
  `_get_sale_order_line_product()`, which here returns `False`; the real kit
  drop-ship/MTO link is supplied by a downstream module (e.g.
  `sale_purchase_mrp` / `mrp_subcontracting`) overriding it — out of scope for
  static facts.
- **`production_group_id` grouping** stamped onto purchase moves when a single
  production group is shared — confirm how it merges receipts feeding one MO.
- **Rounding edge cases** in `_round_last_line_done` / `_get_valuation_price_and_qty`
  (the anglo-saxon "total valuation is zero" `UserError`) under partial receipts
  and returns (`to_refund`).
- **`BomOverviewLine.prototype` patch** (the one JS file) — the buy-route line
  rendering in the BoM Overview client is invisible to the Python facts.
