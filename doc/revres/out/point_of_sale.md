# point_of_sale — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow (code-explorer + code-architect): facts from
> `extract_module.py` / `extract_frontend.py` + code-read of the models. Odoo 19.0.*

The **Point of Sale** module (`point_of_sale`, category *Sales/Point of Sale*,
`application=True`) handles "checkouts and payments for shops and restaurants" —
the in-store till. It is **not** auto-installed and is a hub of its own
subsystem (the `pos_*` family). Its true center of gravity is split: a backend
ORM (`pos.session` envelopes a day of `pos.order`s rung against a `pos.config`
terminal) **and** a standalone, offline-first **OWL single-page app** served at
`/pos/ui`. The Python depends on `stock_account` (sell → deliver + value),
accounting models (via `pos.load.mixin` extensions of `account.*`), `barcodes`,
`iot_base` (hardware), and partner/phone autocomplete.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `stock_account` | Sales decrement inventory (stock.picking) and post stock valuation at closing. |
| `barcodes` | Barcode scanning of products/lots at the counter (barcode.rule extension). |
| `iot_base` | Drive hardware — receipt printer, cash drawer, scale — via an IoT/proxy. |
| `digest` | Surface POS sales as a periodic digest KPI (`kpi_pos_total`). |
| `phone_validation` / `partner_autocomplete` | Capture/validate customer contact at point of sale. |
| `html_editor` | Rich receipt header/footer and product descriptions. |

**Adds on top:** an offline cashier app, cash-controlled till sessions, multi-
method payment (cash/card terminal/QR/pay-later), receipts/invoices, refunds,
and a consolidated accounting closing entry per session.

| **`pos.order`** | Point of Sale Orders | ~61 | `session_id→pos.session`, `partner_id→res.partner`, `account_move→account.move`, `picking_ids→stock.picking` |
| **`pos.order.line`** | Point of Sale Order Lines | ~35 | `order_id→pos.order`, `product_id→product.product`, `combo_parent_id→pos.order.line` |
| **`pos.session`** | Point of Sale Session | ~30 | `config_id→pos.config`, `order_ids→pos.order`, `move_id→account.move`, `statement_line_ids→account.bank.statement.line` |
| **`pos.config`** | Point of Sale Configuration | ~98 | `picking_type_id→stock.picking.type`, `journal_id→account.journal`, `payment_method_ids→pos.payment.method` |
| `pos.payment` | Point of Sale Payments | 25 | `pos_order_id→pos.order`, `payment_method_id→pos.payment.method` |

**Central models:** `pos.order` (the transaction) under `pos.session` (the
cash/accounting envelope) under `pos.config` (the till profile). 55 models total
but **15 are pure `_inherit` extensions** that bolt POS fields onto `account.*`,
`product.*`, `stock.*`, `res.*` — and almost every loaded model mixes in
`pos.load.mixin` (the contract for pushing data into the offline client). The
field mix is **relational + flag-heavy**: Boolean (132) and Many2one (111)
dominate; `pos.config` alone is ~42 Booleans, many of them `module_pos_*`
install-triggers.

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** 8 path groups. `/pos/web`/`/pos/ui` (+`/pos/ui/<config_id>`) load
  the **terminal SPA** (`auth=user`, http); `/pos/service-worker.js` powers the
  offline shell; `/pos/ping` is a jsonrpc heartbeat; `/pos_customer_display/...`,
  `/pos/ticket`, `/pos/ticket/validate` are **public** (customer display +
  receipt lookup). The real RPC surface, though, is the generic ORM call
  `pos.order.sync_from_ui`, not a dedicated route.
- **Frontend (gap #3 — the headline):** the whole UI is an OWL app —
  **166 JS files / 78 XML templates**, ~43 components in
  `point_of_sale._assets_pos` (entry `static/src/app/main.js` + `pos_app.js`,
  root `Chrome`), ~30 registered services/screens (`ProductScreen`,
  `PaymentScreen`, `ReceiptScreen`, `TicketScreen`, `LoginScreen`), a
  **client-side ORM** (`models/related_models/`) persisting to **IndexedDB**, and
  dedicated `pos-offlineErrorHandler`/`pos-rpcErrorHandler`. None of this client
  logic is reverse-engineerable from Python metadata.
- **Views:** backend is list (16) / form (14) / kanban (5) + pivot/graph — the
  config/back-office side; the operational UX lives in the SPA, not in views.
- **Security:** 54 access rules, 12 record rules, **3 groups**
  (`group_pos_user`, `group_pos_manager`, `group_pos_preset`).
- **Integrations (gap #7):** no outbound HTTP/SDK in core; the only endpoint is
  the marketing URL in `__manifest__.py`. Payment-terminal and fiscal
  integrations live in optional `pos_*` bridge modules.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Primary model: Value Chain (transform).** POS is a chain **primary** activity:
its core act is the retail **sell/checkout** that converts demand into a paid
`pos.order`, then drives the chain's outbound steps — delivery (`stock.picking`)
and revenue/cash recognition (`account.move` at session close). It is **not**
support. Caveat: POS has unusually strong **shop** (a service-encounter UI at the
counter) and **network** (an offline terminal mediating buyer ↔ payment
infrastructure) overtones because of its offline-first OWL architecture — but the
value logic is chain.

## APQC PCF Hint

**3.0 Market and Sell Products and Services** — the in-store realization of the
3.5 "manage sales orders" family (the till *is* the order desk), mirroring
`sale`. The triggered side-effects reach **4.0 Deliver Physical Products**
(stock delivery) and **9.0 Manage Financial Resources** (AR + cash at session
close), but those are downstream of the sale, not POS's primary mission.

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill (`.claude/skills/run-odoo/`) with an `-i point_of_sale`
database:

```python
# odoo shell
print(env['pos.config'].search([]).mapped(('name', 'current_session_state')))
ss = env['pos.session'].search([], limit=5)
print(ss.mapped(('name', 'state', 'cash_register_balance_end_real')))
o = env['pos.order'].search([], limit=5)
print(o.mapped(('name', 'state', 'amount_total', 'to_invoice')))
# heartbeat:  curl .../pos/ping  (auth=user, jsonrpc)
# launch UI:  browse /pos/ui/<config_id>
```
