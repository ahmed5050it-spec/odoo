# sale_management — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` + the
> `PROMPT.md` template, deepened with code-read behavioral notes.*

The **Sales** application bundle (`sale_management`, category *Sales/Sales*,
`application=True`) is the app layer that sits on top of the `sale` order engine:
it adds reusable **quotation templates**, customer-selectable **optional
products**, and the digest KPI + **Sales app menu** that surface the application.
It is **not** auto-installed and is thin by design (1570 py LOC, but mostly
glue): it depends on `sale` (the order engine it extends) and `digest` (the
periodic email it feeds). Its center of gravity is the
`sale.order.template` / `sale.order.template.line` pair — the reusable blueprint
that materialises a quotation's lines in one click.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `sale` | The quotation/order engine; this module extends `sale.order` / `sale.order.line` and reuses `sale.report`. |
| `digest` | Adds the `kpi_all_sale_total` sales KPI and tips to the periodic digest email. |

**Adds on top:** quotation templates with sections/optional lines, one-click
template → quotation instantiation, portal self-service for optional products, a
per-company default template + settings feature gate, and the activation of the
**Sales** application menu itself (via init hooks).

## Data Model (the ERM)

| Model (`_name`) | Description | #fields | Key relations |
|-----------------|-------------|--------:|---------------|
| **`sale.order.template`** | Quotation Template | 12 | `sale_order_template_line_ids→sale.order.template.line`, `mail_template_id→mail.template`, `journal_id→account.journal` |
| **`sale.order.template.line`** | Quotation Template Line | 11 | `sale_order_template_id→sale.order.template`, `product_id→product.product`, `parent_id→sale.order.template.line` (sections) |
| `sale.order` *(extension)* | — | +1 | `sale_order_template_id→sale.order.template` |
| `sale.order.line` *(extension)* | — | +1 | adds `is_optional` |
| `digest.digest` *(extension)* | — | +2 | adds `kpi_all_sale_total` |

**Central model:** `sale.order.template` — the template owns its lines and is
referenced from `sale.order` and `res.company` (default template). The three
`_inherit`-only models (`sale.order`, `sale.order.line`, `digest.digest`, plus
`res.company`/`res.config.settings`) are **extensions**, not new entities. The
set is **relational-heavy** (`Many2one` dominates) with a section/subsection
self-reference on the template line.

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** 1 — `/my/orders/<int:order_id>/update_line_dict`
  (`auth='public'`, `type='jsonrpc'`): the portal endpoint where a customer
  adds/removes/sets the quantity of an **optional** order line on their own
  quotation (the `sale.order.option` add-to-order equivalent).
- **Views:** form (2), list (2), search (1) — a small backend surface for
  editing templates; most UX is inherited from `sale`.
- **Security:** 5 access rules, **1 record rule**, 1 group
  (`group_sale_order_template`, the feature gate for Quotation Templates).
- **Frontend:** 4 JS files, 0 OWL; registers a `so_template_line_o2m` field
  widget and a `sale_update_line_button` portal interaction; patches
  `SaleOrderLineListRenderer` / `SaleOrderLineProductField`.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Primary model: Value Chain (transform), front-end app layer.**
`sale_management` is the application bundle that turns the order engine into the
**Sales** app — squarely the *Marketing & Sales* primary activity of the chain.
It does not create a new downstream document of its own: the confirmed
`sale.order` it helps build flows downstream to delivery (`stock`) and invoicing
(`account`) via the parent `sale` engine. Its contribution is **quotation
building** (templates, optional upsell) — a **primary** activity, not support.

## APQC PCF Hint

**3.0 Market and Sell Products and Services** — specifically *manage sales
orders / quotations* (APQC 3.5.3, e.g. 3.5.3.14 revise/prepare quote). The
template-driven prepayment/signature settings touch the cash boundary (9.0) but
delegate to the parent engine.

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill (`.claude/skills/run-odoo/`). With the server up and an
`-i sale_management` database:

```python
# odoo shell
tpls = env['sale.order.template'].search([])
print(tpls.mapped('name'), tpls.mapped('number_of_days'))
# instantiate a quotation from a template (the core onchange):
so = env['sale.order'].new({
    'partner_id': env['res.partner'].search([], limit=1).id,
    'sale_order_template_id': tpls[:1].id,
})
so._onchange_sale_order_template_id()
print(so.order_line.mapped(('product_id', 'product_uom_qty', 'is_optional')))
# portal optional-line endpoint (auth=public, needs access_token + optional line_id):
#   curl .../my/orders/<id>/update_line_dict  (jsonrpc)
```
