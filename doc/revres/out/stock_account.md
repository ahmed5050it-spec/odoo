# stock_account — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` +
> `extract_frontend.py`, behavior from code-read, narrative per `PROMPT.md`.
> Odoo 19.0.*

The **WMS Accounting** module (`stock_account`, category *Supply Chain/Inventory*,
`application=False`) is the **bridge** that wires inventory into the books. It is
**auto-installed** whenever both `stock` and `account` are present and defines no
end-user value stream of its own. Its job is to put a **value** on every stock
movement — using a cost method (standard / FIFO / AVCO) — and, under perpetual
valuation, to post that value to the General Ledger and recognise **Cost of Goods
Sold** against revenue when invoicing. It is the module that makes the product
value-lifecycle's COGS and inventory valuation *real in accounting*.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `stock` | The inventory chain it values: `stock.move`, `stock.quant`, `stock.lot`, locations. |
| `account` | The support ledger it posts to: `account.move`, `account.move.line`, journals, accounts. |

**Adds on top:** cost-method valuation of moves (standard/FIFO/AVCO), perpetual
(real_time) stock journal entries, anglo-saxon COGS lines on invoices, lot-level
valuation, a manual revaluation audit trail, and two valuation reports. It is a
**pure bridge** — `auto_install=true`, no routes, depends on exactly the two hubs
it connects.

| `account.move` *(extension)* | adds `stock_move_ids` | +1 | `stock_move_ids→stock.move` |

**Center of gravity:** the module owns only 3 real models; its weight is in **21
inherit-only extensions** that thread valuation fields through `stock.move`,
`stock.quant`, `stock.lot`, `product.*`, `res.company`, `account.move(.line)`.
Valuation now lives **on `stock.move`** (`value`, `remaining_qty`,
`remaining_value`) — there is **no `stock.valuation.layer`** model in Odoo 19;
`product.value` keeps the manual-adjustment/revaluation history.

## Behavior & Surfaces

Metadata cannot show the wiring; reading the code reveals the bridge:

- **`stock.move._action_done → _set_value() + _create_account_move()`**
  (`models/stock_move.py`): validating a picking values outgoing then incoming
  moves, then for storable **real_time** moves with a valuation account posts an
  `account.move` on the company **stock journal** (debit destination valuation
  account / credit stock valuation account, reversed for outgoing).
- **Cost methods** (`models/product.py`, `stock_move.py`): driven by
  `product.category.property_cost_method` — `standard` = std price × qty; `fifo`
  = `_run_fifo()` consuming a stack of prior incoming moves at their real entry
  cost; `average` = `_update_standard_price()` weighted-average recompute on each
  receipt.
- **`account.move._post → _stock_account_prepare_realtime_out_lines_vals()`**
  (`models/account_move.py`): posting a customer invoice injects two
  `display_type='cogs'` journal items per eligible line (debit COGS/expense,
  credit stock interim) valued via `_get_cogs_value()` — the **anglo-saxon** COGS
  recognition; lines are removed on draft/cancel.
- **`product.product._change_standard_price`** (`models/product.py`): a manual
  standard-price change records a `product.value` row and propagates the
  revaluation — the audit trail behind the AVCO Justifier.
- **Routes:** none. **Views:** form (1), list (1). **Frontend:** 8 JS / 4 OWL
  components — a client-action **Stock Valuation** report
  (`StockValuationReport*`); `web.assets_backend` only, no portal.
- **Integrations:** none (`http_call_sites=0`, no SDKs, no API keys).
- **Security:** 9 access rules, 2 record rules (multi-company scoping), 1 group
  (`stock.group_stock_manager` gates valuation visibility).

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Support — the bridge chain(stock) ↔ support(account).** `stock_account` is not
a primary activity; it is the wiring that lets the value **chain's** inventory
(`stock`) be measured and posted by the **support** accounting function
(`account`). It *realises inventory VALUE*: every move gets a cost, perpetual
valuation hits the stock journal, and COGS is matched to revenue at invoicing —
exactly the mechanics that make the product value-lifecycle's COGS/valuation real
in the books. `auto_install=true` and the absence of any own value stream confirm
its **support** role and **support** activity class.

## APQC PCF Hint

**9.0 Manage Financial Resources** — specifically inventory accounting:
perpetual valuation entries, COGS recognition, and revaluation. It also serves
the inventory-accounting facet of **4.x Deliver Products and Services** (valuing
`stock.quant`/`stock.move` under 4.3.2 *Manage raw material inventory*), but its
center of gravity is the financial-resources/GL side.

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill (`.claude/skills/run-odoo/`). With a database that has
`stock_account` installed (auto-installed with `stock` + `account`):

```python
# odoo shell
# cost method + valuation policy per category
env['product.category'].search([], limit=5).mapped(
    ('name', 'property_cost_method', 'property_valuation'))
# valued moves and their posted GL entry
env['stock.move'].search([('is_valued', '=', True)], limit=5).mapped(
    ('reference', 'value', 'account_move_id'))
# the anglo-saxon COGS lines
env['account.move.line'].search([('display_type', '=', 'cogs')], limit=5).mapped(
    ('account_id', 'balance'))
```
