# pos_online_payment — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow (code-explorer + code-architect): facts from
> `extract_module.py` / `extract_frontend.py` + code-read of the models and the
> payment controller. Odoo 19.0.*

The **Point of Sale online payment** module (`pos_online_payment`, author Odoo
S.A., LGPL-3, `application=False`, **`auto_install=True`**) is a thin bridge that
lets a POS customer **pay a `pos.order` online** — typically by scanning a
link/QR and settling on their own phone through a configured payment provider —
so the till transaction can be marked paid without a physical card terminal. It
declares **no new model** (7 `_inherit` extensions only) and adds **3 public
routes**; its weight (~1296 py LOC) is in method overrides that glue the
`point_of_sale` and `account_payment`/`payment` subsystems together.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why |
|------------|-----|
| `point_of_sale` | The till it extends: `pos.order`, `pos.payment`, `pos.payment.method`, `pos.config`, `pos.session`. |
| `account_payment` | Settlement into `account.payment` and the underlying `payment` engine (providers, transactions). |

**Adds on top:** an "online" `pos.payment.method` type, a public payment portal
for a POS order, and the server-side feedback that turns a confirmed
`payment.transaction` into a real `pos.payment` line and finalises the order.

## Data Model (the ERM)

| Model (`_inherit`) | Adds | Key relations |
|--------------------|------|---------------|
| **`payment.transaction`** | `pos_order_id` | `→ pos.order` |
| **`pos.order`** | `online_payment_method_id` (computed), `next_online_payment_amount` | `→ pos.payment.method` |
| **`pos.payment.method`** | `is_online_payment`, `online_payment_provider_ids` (m2m), `type='online'` | `→ payment.provider` |
| **`pos.payment`** | `online_account_payment_id` (one2one) | `→ account.payment` |
| **`account.payment`** | `pos_order_id` | `→ pos.order` |
| `pos.config` | constraints only (≤1 online method, currency) | — |
| `pos.session` | helper overrides | — |

**Central binding:** a confirmed `payment.transaction(pos_order_id)` ⇒
`account.payment` ⇒ a `pos.payment(online_account_payment_id)` line on the order.
This 1:1 online-payment ↔ account-payment link is enforced by `pos.payment`
create/write guards.

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes (all `auth='public'`):** `/pos/pay/<id>` (http) renders the payment
  form; `/pos/pay/transaction/<id>` (jsonrpc) creates the `payment.transaction`;
  `/pos/pay/confirmation/<id>` (http) is the landing page that settles the
  payment into the POS. Public auth is deliberate — an unauthenticated phone can
  pay via a shared link/QR, gated only by the order `access_token` and an
  **opened** `pos.session`.
- **Settlement (the headline, code-read):**
  `payment.transaction._process_pos_online_payment` (called at confirmation and
  from the inherited async `_post_process`) mints the `account.payment`, resolves
  or creates the online `pos.payment.method`, writes the `pos.payment` line via
  `pos_order.add_payment`, finalises a still-draft paid order with
  `_process_saved_order`, and fires `pos.config._notify(
  'ONLINE_PAYMENTS_NOTIFICATION')` on the bus so the cashier terminal refreshes.
- **Frontend (gap #3):** the cashier UX is OWL/JS — **9 JS / 1 XML**, one
  `OnlinePaymentPopup` component, and patches to `PaymentScreen`,
  `OrderPaymentValidation`, `PosStore`, `PosOrder`, `PosPayment`,
  `CustomerDisplay` and `CustomerDisplayPosAdapter` (the customer-facing
  link/QR). The screen flow and bus-driven "mark paid" logic live in this client
  code, invisible to Python metadata.
- **Views:** none of its own (config widgets injected into POS settings/payment-
  method forms via XML).
- **Security:** no module-owned groups/rules; reuses POS groups and public/portal
  access for the pay routes.
- **Integrations (gap #7):** no direct outbound HTTP/SDK here — acquirer calls
  and api-keys live in the underlying `payment` / `payment_<provider>` modules;
  this module only selects compatible `payment.provider` records.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Value Chain (transform), primary.** The module exists to **complete a retail
sell/checkout** — moving the tender step of an in-store `pos.order` onto the
customer's device. It is classified with its parent `point_of_sale` as a chain
primary activity. The payment-engine it leans on is itself network/support
(collect-cash), and the confirmed transaction does feed an `account.payment`, but
those are downstream effects; the dominant operation here is the POS sale. Strong
**network** overtones (it brokers buyer ↔ acquirer through public mediation
routes), but the value logic it completes is the chain sell.

## APQC PCF Hint

**3.0 Market and Sell Products and Services** — omnichannel payment collection
for the in-store sale (the 3.5 "manage sales orders" family realised at the
till), mirroring `point_of_sale`. The settlement side touches **9.5** (process
payments / collect cash) via `account.payment`, but as a triggered effect.

## Fit-to-Standard

- **Out of the box:** one online method per till backed by configured providers
  (empty = all published); a public pay portal usable from a phone; redirect /
  inline / saved-token flows reused from the payment engine; automatic settlement
  into `pos.payment` + `account.payment`; bus notification to the terminal;
  server-side amount/currency re-validation.
- **Typical fits:** scan-to-pay at the counter; split an order so part is paid
  online (`next_online_payment_amount`); restaurant pay-online with
  `pos_restaurant`; any provider already supported by an installed
  `payment_<provider>` module.
- **Common gaps:** ships **no acquirer** (a `payment_<provider>` module must be
  installed and a provider published/enabled); all providers must share the POS
  config currency; bespoke kiosk/table-QR delivery UX is OWL dev; no extra
  fee/reconciliation logic beyond `account_payment`; country-specific fiscal
  capture may need certification add-ons.
