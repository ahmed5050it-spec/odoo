# project — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` +

> `project_task_type.py`, `project_task_recurrence.py`.*

The **Project** module (`project`, category *Services/Project*,
`application=True`, version 1.4) organizes and plans projects and the tasks
inside them. Unlike the linear quote-to-cash chain, project is a **value-shop**:
it marshals people and resources around solving each engagement as a distinct
problem. Its center of gravity is the `project.project` / `project.task` pair,
with `project.task` carrying ~73 fields and **142 methods** — a workflow-rich
model whose behavior lives almost entirely in Python, not metadata. It depends
on `mail`, `portal`, `rating`, `resource`, `analytic`, and `digest`, and is a
hub other Services modules (timesheets, helpdesk, field service) build upon.

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |

| `rating` | Customer satisfaction ratings on tasks/projects — the reputation driver. |

**Central model:** `project.task` — it self-references three ways (subtasks via
`parent_id`/`child_ids`, dependencies via `depend_on_ids`/`dependent_ids`,
recurrence chain). This recursive self-relation is the structural signature of a
cyclical problem-solving model. Stages (`project.task.type`) are configurable
and carry their own rating + mail templates.

## Behavior & Surfaces

Read from code (the metadata gap):

- **Stage change is an event, not a write.** Setting `stage_id` stamps
  `date_last_stage_update`, emails the stage's `mail_template_id`, and — when the
  stage is `rating_active` with `rating_status='stage'` — fires
  `_send_task_rating_mail(force_send=True)` (task line 1346).
- **Ratings loop back into state.** `rating_apply` writes the task to
  `03_approved` (rating ≥ satisfied) or `02_changes_requested` when the stage has
  `auto_validation_state` (lines 2125-2133) — the solve/evaluate cycle.
- **Recurrence regenerates work.** `_create_next_occurrences_values` copies the
  task and recursively re-creates its subtasks with dates shifted by the
  recurrence delta — the cyclical task/subtask loop.
- **State machine drives chatter.** `_track_subtype` maps six task states to
  distinct mail subtypes (done, canceled, in_progress, approved,
  changes_requested, waiting).
- **Periodic reputation polling.** A cron (`_send_rating_all`) walks
  `rating_status='periodic'` stages and sends rating mails on a deadline.

**Surfaces:** 9 routes (`/my/projects`, `/my/tasks`, `project_sharing`,
attachment upload), 6 security groups, 55 ACLs / 31 record rules, and a wide view
set (16 forms, 22 lists, 12 kanbans, pivot/graph/calendar/activity).

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Value model: `shop`** (Stabell & Fjeldstad value shop / intensive technology).
Project does not push a fixed throughput line; it organizes resources to solve a
unique engagement, cycling through problem-finding (task creation/triage), choice
and execution (stages, subtasks, dependencies), and evaluation (ratings, milestone
checks) — and loops back when changes are requested. **Activity class: primary**
(it is the service-delivery activity itself). **Value driver: reputation** — the
rating mixin, per-stage and periodic rating mails, and `rating_avg` make perceived
solution quality, not unit cost, the basis of value.

## APQC PCF Hint

**Category 5.0 — Deliver Services.** The task lifecycle + milestone + rating
machinery maps to service-delivery / case-work activities: open the engagement,
plan and stage the work, execute through subtasks, validate against customer
satisfaction, and close on milestone completion. Cost is anchored via the
`account.analytic.account` link, tying delivery to project profitability. (See
`apqc_odoo_map.tsv`: `5.x Deliver services (engagement) → project.task`.)
