# sale_mrp — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres code-explorer + code-architect workflow: structural

The **Sales and MRP Management** module (`sale_mrp`, category *Sales/Sales*,
`application=False`, **`auto_install=True`**) is the three-way bridge whose own
manifest says it "keeps track of production orders generated from sales order …
adds sales name and sales Reference on production order." It declares **no model
of its own** — 8 `_inherit`-only classes, ~6.0k Python LOC, ~89 XML LOC — and
sits one layer above `sale_stock`: where `sale_stock` turns a confirmed order
into a delivery, `sale_mrp` makes that delivery *manufacturable* and makes
**kit (phantom-BoM) products** explode into their components on the sale order.
It is the glue that lets a `sale.order` drive an `mrp.production` and lets a sold
kit fulfill, invoice, and cost from its bill of materials.

`auto_install=True` means it activates automatically whenever **both** `mrp` and

### Code-read (the metadata gap)

The static facts say only "thin module"; the real cross-model side-effects are:

- **Kit (phantom BoM) explosion is the core.** A sold product with a
  `type='phantom'` BoM is *exploded* into its components: `sale.order.line.
  _get_bom_component_qty` calls `bom.explode()`; `_get_qty_procurement` and
  `_prepare_qty_delivered` re-derive ordered/delivered quantities from the
  component `move_ids` via `_compute_kit_quantities` (with in/out filters that
  flip perspective because moves are seen from the *sale* document). The ATP
  inventory widget is deliberately **hidden for kits** (`_compute_qty_to_deliver`
  sets `display_qty_widget=False`) because forecasting can't represent them.
- **Manufacture (MTO) traceability: SO line → MO.** When a sold product is
  produced, `stock.rule._prepare_mo_vals` copies `values['sale_line_id']` onto
  the new `mrp.production`, so every manufacturing order knows its originating
  `sale.order.line`. `mrp.production.action_confirm` then propagates that
  `sale_line_id` onto the MO's finished move, and `_get_backorder_mo_vals`
  carries it to backorders — keeping the SO↔MO chain intact through splits.
- **BoM is locked while sold.** `mrp.bom.write`/`unlink` call
  `_ensure_bom_is_free`, which raises `UserError` if any *open* SO line
  (`state='sale'`, not fully invoiced, with live moves) still references the
  kit's product — you cannot delete or un-phantom a BoM mid-fulfillment.
- **Kit costing & pricing.** `stock.move._get_cogs_price_unit` recomputes COGS
  by exploding the kit BoM and weighting each storable component's cost;
  `_get_price_unit` and `stock.move.line._compute_sale_price` value kit moves
  from the component/list price rather than the (zero-cost) phantom parent.

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill (`.claude/skills/run-odoo/`). There are **0 routes to
curl**; drive it through `odoo shell` on the inherited models. Confirm a sale
order for a manufactured/kit product, then read the SO↔MO links:

```python
# odoo shell — sale orders that spawned manufacturing orders
sos = env['sale.order'].search([('mrp_production_count', '>', 0)])
print(sos.mapped(lambda s: (s.name, s.mrp_production_ids.mapped('name'))))

# reverse link: each MO points back to its originating sale order line
mos = env['mrp.production'].search([('sale_line_id', '!=', False)])
print(mos.mapped(lambda m: (m.name, m.sale_line_id.order_id.name, m.state)))

# the kit definitions this module cares about (phantom BoMs)
kits = env['mrp.bom'].search([('type', '=', 'phantom')])
print(kits.mapped(('display_name', 'product_qty')))
```

To see kit behavior end to end: add a phantom-BoM product to a quotation,
confirm it, and inspect `so.order_line.move_ids.mapped('product_id.display_name')`
— the moves are the *components*, not the kit. To see MTO: sell a
manufacture-route product and read `so.mrp_production_ids`.

## Open Questions

- **`stock.reference` walk.** `mrp_production_ids` is computed through
  `sale.order.stock_reference_ids.production_ids` and filtered to
  `not production_group_id.parent_ids` — how multi-level BoMs and shared
  procurement groups populate `stock.reference` (defined in `stock`/`sale_stock`)
  is not visible in this module's facts.
- **Kit delivered-qty edge cases.** `_prepare_qty_delivered` has an explicit
  fallback "when the product sold is made only of kits" (no relevant BoM on the
  moves) — the correctness of that all-or-nothing branch needs live verification.
- **COGS drift.** `_get_cogs_price_unit` warns in-code that a BoM changed
  *between* move creation and invoice validation yields a wrong price; the
  mitigation (reading the move's stored `bom_line_id.bom_id`) should be confirmed.
- **The empty `account.move.line` stub** (`pass`) — present but adds nothing;
  likely a load-order placeholder or vestige worth confirming against history.
