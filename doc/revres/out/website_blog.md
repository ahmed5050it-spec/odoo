# website_blog — Reverse-Engineering Brief

> *Generated by the revres workflow: facts from `extract_module.py` +
> `extract_frontend.py`, code-read behavior, and the `PROMPT.md` template.*

The **Blog** module (`website_blog`, category *Website/Website*,
`application=False`) lets users publish blog posts, announcements and news on the
website front end as a **content-marketing** channel. Its center of gravity is
`blog.post` (~20 fields, 15 methods) — the editorial document — grouped under
`blog.blog` containers and classified by `blog.tag` / `blog.tag.category`. It
depends on `website_mail` (commenting + follower notifications), `website_partner`
(author/partner on the site) and `html_builder` (drag-and-drop content editing).

## Role & Dependencies

| Depends on | Why (inferred) |
|------------|----------------|
| `website_mail` | Posts are `mail.thread`s: publish announces to blog followers; readers comment as `mail.message`s. |
| `website_partner` | Author bylines and partner exposure on the public site (`author_id → res.partner`). |
| `html_builder` | Rich-content editing of the post body and cover, plus the `s_blog_posts` page-builder snippet. |

**Adds on top:** blogs/posts/tags, publication + publication-date gating, SEO &
OpenGraph article metadata, an Atom feed, tag/archive navigation, per-post visit
counting, and reader comments.

## Data Model (the ERM)

| Model (`_name`) | Description | #fields | Key relations |
|-----------------|-------------|--------:|---------------|
| **`blog.post`** | Blog Post | 20 | `blog_id→blog.blog`, `author_id→res.partner`, `tag_ids→blog.tag`, `website_message_ids→mail.message` |
| **`blog.blog`** | Blog | 7 | `blog_post_ids→blog.post` |
| `blog.tag` | Blog Tag | 4 | `category_id→blog.tag.category`, `post_ids→blog.post` (M2M) |
| `blog.tag.category` | Blog Tag Category | 2 | `tag_ids→blog.tag` |

**Central model:** `blog.post`. It mixes in `mail.thread`,
`website.seo.metadata`, `website.published.multi.mixin`,
`website.page_visibility_options.mixin`, `website.cover_properties.mixin` and
`website.searchable.mixin` — so publication, SEO, cover image, visibility and
site-search behavior are inherited rather than redefined. The module also
*extends* `website` and `website.snippet.filter` (2 inherit-only classes).

## Behavior & Surfaces

- **Routes:** 4 route groups, all `type='http'`, `auth='public'`,
  `website=True` — the index/tag/pager family (`/blog`, `/blog/page/<n>`,
  `/blog/tag/<tag>`, `/blog/<blog>` …), the post page
  `/blog/<blog>/<post>`, the Atom `/blog/<blog>/feed`, and a pre-v14 redirect.
- **Publish (code-read):** `blog.post.write/create` → `_check_for_publication`
  stamps `published_date=now()` on first publish, force-unpublishes on archive,
  and calls `blog_id.message_post_with_source('blog_post_template_new_post',
  subtype mt_blog_blog_published)` — publishing **announces the post to the
  blog's followers**.
- **SEO/social (code-read):** `_default_website_meta` emits OpenGraph/Twitter
  article cards (`og:type=article`, `article:published_time/modified_time/tag`,
  `og:image` from cover properties); `teaser` is the first 200 chars of content
  unless `teaser_manual` is set; `website_url = /blog/<slug>/<slug>`.
- **Visit tracking (code-read):** the post controller calls
  `sql.increment_fields_skiplock(post,'visits')` once per browser session
  (`request.session['posts_viewed']`) and computes `next_post` for sequential
  reading.
- **Comments (code-read):** `_mail_post_access='read'` lets any read-access user
  comment; `website_message_ids` is filtered to `message_type='comment'`
  (10/page); inbox notifications are suppressed for comments and reply-emails are
  downgraded to internal notes to avoid spamming followers.
- **Frontend (static):** 15 JS / 5 XML, **0 OWL** — behavior ships as website
  `public.interactions` (`blog_posts`, `blog_post_link`, `website_blog`, plus
  edit/preview variants) and two builder patches (`CoverPropertiesOption`,
  `NewContentSystrayItem`).
- **Security:** 16 ACLs, **2 record rules**, **0 own groups** — CRUD reserved to
  `website.group_website_designer`; public/portal read **published/active** only.

## Value-Configuration Classification

**Primary model: Value Network (mediating), primary activity.** `website_blog`
is a network-style mediating activity (Stabell & Fjeldstad): it **publishes
content to attract and engage an audience**, brokering the relationship between
the organization and its readership through mediating infrastructure — public
blog pages, tag navigation, SEO/OpenGraph metadata, an Atom feed, visit tracking,
publish-time follower announcements, and a reader-comment channel. It performs
the network firm's **network promotion** function (recruiting and serving an
audience network) rather than transforming inputs (chain) or solving a bespoke
client problem (shop). It is a **primary** marketing activity — the inbound
content-marketing channel — not internal support.

## How to Drive It

Use the `run-odoo` skill (`.claude/skills/run-odoo/`). With the server up and an
`-i website_blog` database:

```python
# odoo shell
posts = env['blog.post'].search([], limit=5)
print(posts.mapped(('name', 'is_published', 'visits', 'post_date')))
print(env['blog.blog'].search([]).mapped(('name', 'blog_post_count')))
# publish an unpublished post -> observe published_date stamp + follower announce:
p = env['blog.post'].search([('is_published', '=', False)], limit=1)
p.write({'is_published': True})
# public: GET /blog (index) ; GET /blog/<blog-slug>/feed (Atom XML)
```
